## WHAT CLASSGRID IS

**Classgrid AI** — an AI agent that does real work. Live at **chat.classgrid.in**, marketing site at **classgrid.in**.

- **Agent mode:** everyday work. Web search, reading files (PDF, Word, PowerPoint, Excel, CSV, images), running Python, charts, documents and reports.
- **Project mode:** a code project open in an editor beside the chat; the agent reads and changes the real files.
- **Studio mode:** agent-first building. The agent writes the code, runs the commands and checks the result while the user watches files and terminal live.
- **Desktop app:** the agent works on folders and the terminal on the user's own computer.
- **Models:** Auto, or 19 models (Claude, DeepSeek, Kimi, GLM, Qwen, Llama and more), switchable mid-chat.
- **Plugins:** 20+ apps through the AI Hub (Gmail, Google Drive, Google Calendar, Notion, Slack, GitHub, Outlook, Teams, Zoom).
- **Schedules:** daily briefs, weekly reports and reminders delivered on WhatsApp.
- **Pricing:** credits from ₹299 to ₹10,000, no fixed plan. Payments are live.

## WHY YC

Classgrid AI is live and works for anyone who signs up. The gap is not features; it is getting the product in front of people. A solo student founder with ₹0 cannot buy that reach.

### 2. Every user costs money before they pay.
An agent spends real model usage on every task. Growing from under 50 users to thousands means paying for usage before revenue catches up.

### 3. One person cannot do everything.
Nikhil builds, runs infrastructure, handles support and attends college. YC funding means hiring help so building doesn't stop while he does everything else.

### 4. Credibility.
The competition is ChatGPT, Claude, Manus, Lovable and Emergent, all funded. YC changes how users, partners and future hires see a 19-year-old solo founder.
