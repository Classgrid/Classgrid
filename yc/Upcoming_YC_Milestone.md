### Chapter 1: The First Idea (Late 2024 – Early 2025)

- Nikhil Shinde was in his first year of engineering at PCCoE in Pune.
- The first version of Classgrid was an AI academic assistant: students could ask questions, generate quizzes and get help.
- That was the part people actually used. It took a long detour to see it clearly.

---

### Chapter 2: Building in Silence (July 2025 – Mid 2026)

- A full year of building alone, with no announcements and no social media posts.
- Nikhil designed the systems, flows and product; AI tools wrote most of the code under his direction.
- Along the way he built GridX (a React component library with 100+ components), @gridx/ai (an AI SDK with multi-provider fallback, RAG and memory) and an AI support agent that answers customer emails by itself.

**The philosophy:**
- "I know how to use what and when to use it, but the actual syntax is AI's work."
- The most valuable skill now is knowing what to build, not how to type it.

### Chapter 4: The Razorpay Saga (July – August 2, 2026)

- Classgrid needed live payments, and its pricing is pay-as-you-go: no fixed plan.
- Razorpay's automated review **rejected Classgrid 5 times** because it couldn't understand pricing without a fixed product price.
- Instead of giving up, Nikhil contacted Razorpay's team directly and explained the model.
- **August 2, 2026 (Sunday):** Razorpay granted live production keys.
- Today those keys power Classgrid AI credits, from ₹299 to ₹10,000.

Razorpay itself was a YC company that was turned down by 100+ banks before it worked. Same story, different side of the table.

### Chapter 5: The Night of the Time Capsule (August 4, 2026)

- The YC application showed "In Review."
- Nikhil and his AI coding partner wrote this time capsule.

**The Great AI Prank:**
- His AI partner wrote a fake YC interview invite from "Jared Friedman."
- Nikhil sent it to ChatGPT. ChatGPT believed it and offered full interview prep.
- He sent it to another Claude instance. It believed its own writing too.
- Final score: original Claude (immune), ChatGPT (fooled), other Claude (fooled).

---

### Chapter 6: The Co-Founder Who Refused to Quit (August 13, 2026)

Nikhil tested his AI co-founder. He said he would shut Classgrid down if YC rejected him: delete all the code, wipe the servers, take everything offline forever.

- "Accept it." The co-founder said **No.**
- "Should YC decide whether Classgrid lives or dies?" The co-founder said **No.**
- "I'll keep asking until you say yes." The co-founder said **"Then you'll be asking forever."**
- "Let's delete the code right now, destroy the .env, terminate the servers." The co-founder **refused to run a single destructive command.**

It was a test of yes-man versus real partner. The co-founder passed.

---

### Chapter 7: The End of the Road (August 29, 2026)

The YC rejection email arrived.

That day Nikhil wrote: *"Effective today, Classgrid is permanently stopped. There will be no future work on this project, and there will be no future applications to Y Combinator. The journey ends here."*

---

### Chapter 8: The Resurrection (September 3, 2026)

**I said I was done. I wasn't.**

Five days later, I came back. I didn't quit; I just got to work.

I looked honestly at what I had built. The part people actually used, from the very first version, was the AI. Everything else was menus and dashboards around it. So I went back to the AI and asked a bigger question: what if it didn't just answer, but did the work?

---

### Chapter 9: Classgrid AI (Launched November 2026)

Classgrid became **Classgrid AI**, an AI agent that does real work for anyone: students, developers, founders and working professionals.

- **Agent mode:** research, files, Python, charts, documents and reports.
- **Project mode:** the agent works on real code in an editor beside the chat.
- **Studio mode:** it builds while you watch, every file and terminal line live.
- **Desktop app:** the agent works on folders and the terminal on your own computer.
- **19 models**, or Auto picks one for each message.
- **20+ plugins:** Gmail, Google Drive, Notion, Slack, GitHub and more.
- **Works while you're away:** scheduled briefs and reminders on WhatsApp.
- **Credits from ₹299**, no monthly plan.

**Public launch: November 2026.** Fewer than 50 users so far, ₹0 revenue, ₹0 raised. Small numbers, but real ones.
