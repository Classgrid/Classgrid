The Classgrid AI agent can work with your Grids chats and groups. You ask from your normal AI chat, and the agent reads, drafts, polls and posts for you. It always checks with you before it sends anything.

## What the agent can do

| You want to… | The agent can… |
|---|---|
| Catch up on a group | Read the recent messages and give you a short summary |
| Make a decision together | Create a poll in a group, then read the results back to you |
| Tell everyone something | Draft a message or announcement and post it after you approve |
| Share a file | Send a file to a chat or a group |
| Find people or groups | List your chats and groups, check members and find people to message |

The agent understands requests about groups in English, Hindi and Hinglish.

## How to ask

Open a chat in Agent mode at [chat.classgrid.in](https://chat.classgrid.in) and just ask. For example:

- "Catch me up on the project team group."
- "Summarise what the project group agreed tonight."
- "Make a poll in the trip group: Goa, Manali or Ooty?"
- "What did people vote in the trip poll?"
- "Draft an announcement for the community group: Sunday's meetup moves to 5 pm."
- "Send the latest proposal PDF to the project group."

## It always asks before posting

Before the agent sends anything to a group, it shows you the draft and asks:

> "Should I send this to the group?"

Nothing is posted until you say yes. You can ask it to change the wording first as many times as you like.

## It asks before reading one to one chats

When you say "read my Grid", the agent looks at your **groups** only. Your one to one chats are more private, so the agent asks you first before it reads any of them.

## It reads recent messages only

The agent reads the **recent** messages in a group, roughly the last 1 to 2 hours. It is great for "what did I miss this evening?" It is not meant for summing up a group's whole history.

<callout type="info">Tip: ask for a catch up soon after a busy discussion, while the messages are still recent.</callout>

## What the agent cannot do

<callout type="warning">The agent cannot post into Grids on a schedule. Messages like "post this every Monday" are not possible. To send a group message later, schedule it yourself with Schedule For in Message Options.</callout>

- **No AI bot inside groups.** The AI does not sit inside a group or answer @ mentions there. You talk to the agent in your own AI chat.
- **No scheduled posts.** The AI's own Schedules send reminders only to you, by email or WhatsApp.
- **Recent history only**, as explained above.

## Want to use the AI together?

If you want several people to talk to the same AI at once, use **Share Grid** instead. It lets up to 4 people work in one AI chat, and the AI knows who asked what. See [Share Grid](/docs/share-grid).

## Example: plan a group trip

1. Ask: "Make a poll in the Friends Trip group: which weekend works, 8th, 15th or 22nd? Allow multiple answers."
2. Check the poll the agent suggests and confirm.
3. Later, ask: "What are the results of the weekend poll?"
4. Ask: "Post the winning weekend in the group and ask everyone to confirm by tonight."
5. Check the draft, then say yes to send.

## Common questions

<details><summary>Will my group members see that the AI wrote the message?</summary>Announcements from the agent appear as normal messages in the group. Always read the draft before you approve it.</details>

<details><summary>Can the agent read every message I have ever received?</summary>No. It reads recent group messages, and it asks before reading any one to one chat.</details>

<details><summary>Can I ask the AI something from inside a group chat?</summary>No. Ask in your own AI chat instead. For a shared AI chat with other people, use Share Grid.</details>
