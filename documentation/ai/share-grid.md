Share Grid turns any normal AI chat into a shared one. Up to 4 people can read the chat, send messages and ask the AI together. Each message shows who sent it, and the AI knows who asked what.

## Open Share Grid

1. Open the AI chat you want to share at [chat.classgrid.in](https://chat.classgrid.in).
2. In the chat header, tap the **Share Grid** icon. (It is an icon with no text label.)
3. The **Invite people** panel opens.

<callout type="warning">Incognito chats and Project Mode chats can't be shared with Share Grid.</callout>

## Invite people

There are three ways to invite someone.

### By email

1. Type one or more email addresses in the box, for example `name@gmail.com, friend@gmail.com`.
2. Choose **Invite**.

They get an email saying you invited them to a chat on Classgrid AI: "You can read the chat, send messages and ask the AI together." The email has an **Accept invite** button.

<callout type="info">An emailed invite only works for that email address. The person must sign in with the same email to join.</callout>

### From your Grids contacts

1. Choose **Add from Grids**.
2. Use **Pick people from your Grids** or **Search people** to find them.
3. Select the people you want to add.

### With an invite link

1. Choose **Copy invite link**.
2. Send the link any way you like, for example in WhatsApp.
3. When you no longer want new people to join with it, choose **Turn off invite link**.

When someone joins with the link, the chat shows "X joined the chat via the invite link".

## How many people

A shared chat holds **up to 4 people**, counting you. The panel shows **People · N of 4**, with how many messages each person has sent.

**History** in the panel lists every invite and its state: Pending, Accepted, Declined or Turned off.

## What the person you invite sees

1. They open the invite and see "You're invited to a Classgrid AI chat".
2. If they are not signed in, they choose **Log in to join**. They need a Classgrid AI account.
3. They choose **Join chat** (or decline, which shows "Invite declined").
4. They choose **Open chat** and see the whole conversation so far.

Shared chats are marked with a star in the chat history, so they are easy to find.

## Working together

- Every message is tagged with the name of the person who sent it.
- The AI knows who asked what, so it can answer each person properly.
- **One person at a time.** While the AI is answering someone, others see "Agent is responding to X. You can send when it's done." Wait a moment, then send.

### Ideas

- Draft a pitch or proposal with your team.
- Work through a hard problem with up to 3 friends.
- Plan a trip with family, with everyone adding ideas.
- Brainstorm content with a collaborator.

## Leaving and ownership

- Anyone can choose **Leave chat** in the panel.
- If you leave a chat someone else shared with you, it is removed from your list.
- If the owner leaves, ownership passes to the next member, so the chat keeps going.

## Common questions

<details><summary>Do the people I invite need an account?</summary>Yes. They need a Classgrid AI account to join. They can sign up for free at chat.classgrid.in.</details>

<details><summary>Can more than 4 people join?</summary>No. A shared chat holds up to 4 people in total. If you need more people, create a group in Grids instead.</details>

<details><summary>Can two people ask the AI at the same time?</summary>No. The AI answers one person at a time. The others see who it is answering and can send when it is done.</details>

<details><summary>Is Share Grid the same as a share link?</summary>No. Share Grid lets people join and talk in the chat. A share link is a public page that people can only read.</details>
