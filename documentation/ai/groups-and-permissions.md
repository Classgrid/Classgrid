Groups let many people talk in one place. You choose the group type, who can find and join it, and what members are allowed to do. Every change is recorded in an audit log that you can download.

## Create a group

1. Open **Grids** from the main menu.
2. Choose **New group**.
3. Enter a **Group name** (required) and a **Group subject** (optional).
4. Pick a group type and a privacy option.
5. Create the group, then add members.

### Group types

When you create a group, you pick a type from the list in the app. Most people use one of these:

| Type | Good for |
|---|---|
| **General** | Friends, family, a trip plan or a hobby group |
| **Team / Staff** | A work team or a project group |
| **Official / Announcement** | An announcement group for a community, where a few people post updates |

The type is a label that helps you and your members. The rules for what people can do come from the permissions below.

## Privacy options

| Option | Who can see it | How people join |
|---|---|---|
| **Private (Hidden, Invite Only)** | Only members | Only when someone allowed to add members adds them |
| **Approval Required (Visible, Request to Join)** | Anyone on Classgrid AI | They send a request, and an admin approves it |
| **Public / Open (Visible, Instant Join)** | Anyone on Classgrid AI | They join right away |

<callout type="info">Groups are open to Classgrid AI users only. People need a Classgrid AI account before they can join.</callout>

## Find and join groups

- Use **Explore Groups** to see groups that are visible to you.
- For an **Approval Required** group, send a request to join.
- Open **Requests** from the main menu to see **My Requests**. When nothing is waiting, it shows "All caught up".

Group admins decide on requests to join an Approval Required group.

## Permissions

Open the group settings to decide who can do what. Each setting has options such as **Only Admins**.

| Setting | What it controls |
|---|---|
| **Who can send messages?** | Set this to Only Admins for an announcement-only group |
| **Who can edit group info?** | Who can change the name, subject and other details |
| **Who can add members?** | Who can bring new people in |
| **Who can send attachments (Images, PDFs, etc)?** | Who can share files and media |

### Example: an announcement group for a community

1. Create a group with the type **Official / Announcement**.
2. Set **Who can send messages?** to **Only Admins**.
3. Post your updates. Members can read them, and you can open **Message Info** to see **Read by** for each update.

## Admins and members

- Use **Add member** to bring people into the group (if the permissions allow you).
- Use **Promote** to make a member an admin.
- The permission settings above decide whether everyone or only admins can send messages, edit group info, add members and send files.

## Audit log

Every group keeps a record of what changed.

1. Open **Audit Logs** from the main menu.
2. Look through the list of changes: member changes, settings changes and message changes.
3. Choose **Export CSV** to download the list as a spreadsheet file.

This is useful when you need to check who changed a setting or who joined.

## Common questions

<details><summary>How do I make a group where only I can post?</summary>Open the group settings and set "Who can send messages?" to Only Admins. Members can still read everything.</details>

<details><summary>Can people from WhatsApp or other apps join my group?</summary>No. Grids groups are for Classgrid AI users. Ask people to sign up at chat.classgrid.in first.</details>

<details><summary>Can the AI post in my group?</summary>Yes, from your AI chat. It always shows you a draft and asks "Should I send this to the group?" first. See AI in Grids.</details>
