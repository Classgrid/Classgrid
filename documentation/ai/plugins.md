Plugins connect Classgrid AI to the apps you already use. Once an app is connected, you can ask the agent to read, search, create and send things in that app, right from your chat. There are 21 plugins today.

## Where to find plugins

1. Sign in at [chat.classgrid.in](https://chat.classgrid.in).
2. In the message box, tap the sliders icon to open the **AI Hub**.
3. Open the **Plugins** tab. You will see **Plugins & Integrations**: "Connect apps to use in Classgrid."
4. Tap a plugin to see what it does and its **Try asking the AI** examples.
5. Tap **Connect**.

For step by step help, see [Connect and disconnect apps](/docs/connect-and-disconnect-apps). You can also browse every plugin on the website at [classgrid.in/plugins](https://classgrid.in/plugins).

## Good to know

Each plugin does a focused set of things:

- **Google Calendar** can list and create events, but can't move, cancel or edit them.
- **Zoom** and **Google Meet** can set up meetings, but can't get recordings or summaries.
- **Vercel** can deploy, but can't roll back or show build logs.
- **Canva** can create, export and resize designs, but can't edit the content inside a design.
- **Instagram Business** needs an image to publish a post. Reels, stories and scheduled posts are not supported.
- **Spotify** playback needs Spotify Premium and an open Spotify app on one of your devices.
- **Outlook** can't delete or move emails.

<callout type="warning">The WhatsApp Business plugin needs your own Meta app details: Phone Number ID, Permanent Access Token (System User) and Webhook Verify Token. You must have a registered WhatsApp Business app in the Meta Developer Console. It sends from your business number, not from the Classgrid AI number.</callout>

## Using a plugin in chat

After you connect an app, just ask in plain words. You can also type **@** in the message box and pick the plugin from the list. The agent can use your connected apps during a [WhatsApp call](/docs/whatsapp-calls) too.

## Common questions

<details><summary>Can I remove a plugin later?</summary>Yes. Open the plugin in the AI Hub and tap Disconnect at any time.</details>

<details><summary>Does the agent act as me in these apps?</summary>Yes. It works through your own account, so an email sent with Gmail comes from your Gmail address.</details>
