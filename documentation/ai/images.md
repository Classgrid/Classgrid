Classgrid AI can make a new image from your words: a poster, a thumbnail, an illustration for a report or a quick idea picture. Describe what you want, and the image appears in the chat.

## How to create an image

### Option 1: the @ menu

1. Type `@` in the message box.
2. Pick **Create image** ("Visualize anything").
3. Describe the image and send.

### Option 2: just ask

Write your request in plain words, for example:

- "Create an image of a cosy reading corner with plants and warm evening light."
- "Make a YouTube thumbnail background for a video about budget travel in Goa."
- "Draw a simple, friendly illustration of a delivery van for my shop's flyer."

The agent makes the image and shows it in the chat. Images you create are also kept in the AI Hub under **Images** ("My Generated Images").

## Ideas

| Use | Try asking |
|---|---|
| **Posters** | "A bright poster background for a music night called Spectrum, neon colours." |
| **Thumbnails** | "A bold thumbnail background with a laptop and coffee, space on the left for text." |
| **Explainers** | "A clean, simple illustration of how solar panels power a home." |
| **Social posts** | "A festive Diwali greeting image with diyas and rangoli, no text." |
| **Mood boards** | "Three different logo mood images for a bakery called Crumb." (ask one at a time) |
| **Stories** | "A watercolour picture of a fox reading a book under a tree." |

## Tips for better images

- **Describe the subject first.** What is in the picture? "A golden retriever wearing a raincoat".
- **Add the setting.** Where is it? "On a rainy Mumbai street at night".
- **Choose a style.** "Photo-realistic", "flat illustration", "watercolour", "3D render", "pencil sketch".
- **Set the mood and colours.** "Warm and cosy", "bright pastel colours", "dark and dramatic".
- **Say how it will be used.** "Wide banner", "square social post", "phone wallpaper", "leave empty space at the top for a title".
- **Keep words out of the picture.** AI images often get text wrong. Ask for "no text" and add your title later, or ask the agent to place text on top using the [code sandbox](/docs/code-and-data).
- **Change it by describing it again.** If the result is not right, ask for a new version with the change, for example "Same idea, but at sunset and with fewer people."

<callout type="info">

Want to add your title, logo or a border to an image? Ask the agent, for example "Put the text 'Spectrum 2026' at the top of this image in white". It can edit images with code in the [code sandbox](/docs/code-and-data).

</callout>

## Using your images

- **Download** the image from the chat.
- **Find it later** in the AI Hub under **Images**.
- **Get it on WhatsApp.** Ask "Send this image to my WhatsApp" once your number is [verified](/docs/verify-whatsapp).
- **Use it in a file.** "Put this image on the cover of a PDF report" or "Add it to a PowerPoint slide". See [Code and data](/docs/code-and-data).
- **Use it on a website.** "Use this image as the hero banner on my site". See [Publish a website](/docs/publish-a-website).

## Limits

Image making uses your credits and has its own usage limit. If you run out, you can top up from **Upgrade** in the AI Hub. See [Credits and top-ups](/docs/credits-and-top-ups).

## FAQ

<details>
<summary>Can I upload a photo and ask questions about it?</summary>

Yes. That is image reading, not image making. See [Files and documents](/docs/files-and-documents).

</details>

<details>
<summary>Can it edit the image it just made?</summary>

The simplest way is to ask for a new version with your change described. For overlays like text, borders or resizing, the agent can work on the image in the [code sandbox](/docs/code-and-data).

</details>

<details>
<summary>Where can I find images I made earlier?</summary>

In the AI Hub under **Images**: "View and download all the images you've generated across all your chat sessions."

</details>
