Classgrid AI can build a website for you and put it live on the internet in one chat. Describe what you want, pick a name, and your site goes live at `yourname.sites.classgrid.in`.

## How it works

1. **Describe the site.** For example: "Build me a simple, modern one-page personal portfolio website."
2. **The agent builds it.** It writes the pages, styles and any images it needs.
3. **Choose a name.** Tell it the name you want, for example "Publish it as priya-designs."
4. **It goes live.** The agent publishes the site and replies with the address, for example "Live at https://priya-designs.sites.classgrid.in".
5. **Open and share.** Click the link to see it, then share it with anyone.

<callout type="info">

Publishing is part of **Agent mode** only. It is not available in Project Mode chats or in tasks started from a WhatsApp call or an email. To put a Project Mode site online, use the Vercel plugin. See [Deploy with Vercel](/docs/deploy-with-vercel).

</callout>

## Site names

Your site lives at `<name>.sites.classgrid.in`.

- **First to publish owns the name.** A site name belongs to whoever publishes it first. If a name is already taken, pick a different one.
- **Keep it short and clear.** Lowercase letters, numbers and dashes are easiest to read and type, for example `crumb-bakery` or `rahul-photo`.
- **Pick it early.** If you have a name in mind, publish soon so it is yours.

## Ideas

| Site | Try asking |
|---|---|
| **Portfolio** | "Make a portfolio site in one chat, with my projects, a short bio and a contact section." |
| **Landing page** | "Build a landing page for your idea: a weekend home-cooked meal delivery service." |
| **Event page** | "Make a page for our team offsite on 14 December with the agenda, venue map link and an RSVP email link." |
| **Small business** | "Build a one-page site for a bakery called Crumb with the menu, opening hours and a WhatsApp order button." |
| **Link page** | "Make a simple page with links to my Instagram, YouTube and online shop." |
| **Trip plan** | "Turn our Goa trip plan into a nice-looking page I can share with friends." |

## Making changes

Keep chatting in the same chat to change your site:

- "Make the header dark blue and the buttons orange."
- "Add a section with three customer reviews."
- "Use this photo as the main banner." (attach the image, see [Files and documents](/docs/files-and-documents))
- "Make it look better on phones."

Then ask the agent to publish again to the same name.

## Tips

- **Give real content.** Paste your bio, services, product list or opening hours. The site will be far better than with made-up text.
- **Share a style.** "Clean and minimal", "bold and colourful", or "like a modern tech startup".
- **Add images.** Upload your own photos, or ask the agent to [create images](/docs/images) for the site.
- **Check it on your phone.** Open the link on your phone and ask for fixes if anything looks off.
- **Plan first for bigger sites.** "Suggest the sections and layout first, then build it after I agree."

<callout type="warning">

Anything you publish is public. Anyone with the link can see it, so do not put private details such as passwords, ID numbers or personal addresses on your site.

</callout>

## FAQ

<details>
<summary>Can I publish from Project Mode?</summary>

No. Classgrid's `sites.classgrid.in` hosting works in Agent mode only. In Project Mode, connect the Vercel plugin and ask the agent to deploy. See [Deploy with Vercel](/docs/deploy-with-vercel).

</details>

<details>
<summary>Can I publish from a WhatsApp call?</summary>

Not to `sites.classgrid.in`. Tasks started from a WhatsApp call or an email can't publish here. Open a chat at chat.classgrid.in and ask there.

</details>

<details>
<summary>Someone already has the name I want. What can I do?</summary>

A site name belongs to whoever published it first, so choose another name, for example by adding a word or your city.

</details>
