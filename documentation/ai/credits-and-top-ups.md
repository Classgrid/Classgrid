Classgrid AI has three plans: **Free**, **Pro** and **Max**. Free costs nothing. Every plan has limits on how much AI work you can do. When you need more, you move to a bigger plan or, on Pro and Max, top up with extra AI credits. This page explains the plans, what uses your limit, how to top up and how to see what you have used.

## The three plans

| Plan | What you get |
|---|---|
| **Free** | Costs nothing. Use the agent within the Free plan's limits. No Claude models. |
| **Pro** | Higher limits, Claude models, more images and more storage. You can top up. |
| **Max** | Higher limits, Claude models, more images and more storage. You can top up. |

Sign up at [chat.classgrid.in](https://chat.classgrid.in) with your email and start chatting on the Free plan. You don't need to pay anything to try the agent.

<callout type="info">Prices and the exact limits for each plan are on [classgrid.in/pricing](https://classgrid.in/pricing). They can change, so always check there for the latest.</callout>

## How plan limits work

Every plan has two limits:

- **A limit that refills every few hours.** When you reach it, you can use the AI again when it comes back. The app shows the time.
- **A weekly limit.** This is the most you can use in a week.

Pro and Max have higher limits than Free.

## What uses your limit

Your plan's limit is used when the AI does work for you. For example:

| What you do | Uses your limit? |
|---|---|
| Chatting with the agent | Yes |
| Making images | Yes |
| WhatsApp calls with Classgrid AI | Yes |
| Tasks the agent does after a call or email | Yes |
| Work the agent does in your connected apps | Yes |

Bigger jobs, longer chats and stronger models use more than short, simple questions. The model you pick can make a difference. See [Choose a model](/docs/choose-a-model).

## Move to Pro or Max

If you are on Free and want more, move to Pro or Max:

1. Sign in at [chat.classgrid.in](https://chat.classgrid.in).
2. Open **Settings** > **Billing**.
3. Choose **Pro** or **Max** and follow the steps to pay.

You can also compare the plans and start from [classgrid.in/pricing](https://classgrid.in/pricing).

## Top up (Pro and Max)

Top-ups are extra AI credits for Pro and Max users. They are used only after your plan's limit runs out, so your work can keep going. Free users can't top up; move to Pro or Max instead.

1. Sign in at [chat.classgrid.in](https://chat.classgrid.in).
2. Open **Settings** > **Billing**.
3. Click **Buy credits**.
4. Choose how much you want to add and follow the steps to pay.
5. Your new credits are added to your account.

Bought credits expire after a set time, so use them soon. See [classgrid.in/pricing](https://classgrid.in/pricing) for the current details.

## Check your usage

Open **Settings** > **Usage** to see how much of your plan's limits you have used and when they come back.

## What happens when you reach your limit

- **In chat**, you see "You've reached your {Plan} plan's limit. It comes back on {time}." On Free you get a **See plans** button. On Pro and Max you get a **Top up** button.
- **On a WhatsApp call**, the call is not taken. On Free you hear "You've used your Free plan's limit for now, so Classgrid AI can't take calls. Calls come back on {time}, or upgrade to Pro or Max for more." On Pro and Max the end of the message says "or top up on chat.classgrid.in".
- **During a long task**, the task pauses and you get a WhatsApp message: "You've used your {Plan} plan's limit for now, so I paused your task. I'll continue by myself when it comes back on {time}." On Free it also says you can upgrade to Pro or Max for more. On Pro and Max it says you can top up on chat.classgrid.in.

<callout type="warning">If you are about to start a big job, like a long research task or a WhatsApp call with many tasks, check **Settings** > **Usage** first so the work doesn't stop halfway.</callout>

## Tips to make your limit go further

- **Use Auto.** The **Auto** model picks the best model for each message.
- **Be clear and specific.** A clear request gets the right answer faster, with fewer back and forth messages.
- **Group related questions** into one message instead of many.
- **Start a new chat for a new topic.** Very long chats carry more history with every message.

## Common questions

<details><summary>Do I need to pay to try Classgrid AI?</summary>No. The Free plan costs nothing. Move to Pro or Max only when you need more.</details>

<details><summary>Can I top up on the Free plan?</summary>No. Top-ups are only for Pro and Max. On Free, wait for your limit to come back or move to Pro or Max from Settings > Billing.</details>

<details><summary>When are top-up credits used?</summary>Only after your plan's limit runs out. Until then, your plan's limit is used first.</details>

<details><summary>Where can I see the prices?</summary>On [classgrid.in/pricing](https://classgrid.in/pricing).</details>

<details><summary>Is there a team or shared plan?</summary>Each person has their own account and their own plan. To work together, use Share Grid or a group in Grids.</details>
