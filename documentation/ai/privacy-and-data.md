You decide what Classgrid AI keeps and what it can reach. This page shows the simple controls you have: deleting chats, Incognito chats, disconnecting apps, how Project Mode keeps your code, and how share links work. For the full legal policy, read our [Privacy Policy](/privacy).

## Delete a chat

1. At [chat.classgrid.in](https://chat.classgrid.in), find the chat in your chat history in the sidebar.
2. Open the chat's menu.
3. Choose **Delete**, then confirm with **Delete chat**.

If someone else shared the chat with you using Share Grid, the menu shows **Leave chat** instead. Leaving removes it from your list. The chat stays with the other people in it.

## Use Incognito for private questions

Incognito chats are not saved.

- Start an **Incognito chat** and you'll see "You're incognito".
- "Incognito chats are not saved to your history or search. They stay in this browser until you close them."
- When you close an Incognito chat, it is deleted.
- Incognito chats can't be shared with Share Grid.

See [Incognito](/docs/incognito) for more.

## Disconnect apps

When you connect an app such as Gmail or Google Drive, the agent can work in it as you. You can remove that access at any time:

1. Open the **AI Hub** (the sliders icon in the message box).
2. Go to **Plugins**.
3. Tap the connected app and choose **Disconnect**.

After that, the agent can no longer use the app. See [Connect and disconnect apps](/docs/connect-and-disconnect-apps).

<callout type="info">Only connect the apps you need. If you connected an app for one job, you can disconnect it as soon as you are done.</callout>

## Project Mode: your code stays on GitHub

[Project Mode](/docs/project-mode) is linked to your GitHub account.

- Your code is kept on **GitHub**. When you open a project, the latest code is downloaded fresh from GitHub.
- While you work, a **temporary copy** sits in your editor so you and the agent can change it. This copy is removed after you close the editor.
- Before it is removed, any work you haven't pushed is saved to a backup branch on GitHub, so nothing is lost. See [Pull requests and backups](/docs/pull-requests-and-backups).
- Deleting a project in Classgrid AI does not delete your code: "Its code stays on GitHub. Only the project here is deleted."

## Share links are public

When you share a chat with a [share link](/docs/share-links), it becomes a **public, read-only page**. Anyone who has the link can read it, without signing in.

<callout type="warning">Before you share a chat, check that it has no private details such as phone numbers, home addresses, passwords, or emails and files from your connected apps.</callout>

**Share Grid** is different. Only the people you invite (up to 4 people in total) can see and use the chat. See [Share Grid](/docs/share-grid).

## Your files

Files you upload and files the AI makes are kept in your [Library](/docs/library), where you can search and manage them.

## WhatsApp

- Calls only work from a WhatsApp number you have verified on your own account.
- Results and reminders from Classgrid AI go only to your own verified WhatsApp.
- Texting the number doesn't start a task. New tasks start from a call or an email to agent@classgrid.in.

See [Call Classgrid AI on WhatsApp](/docs/whatsapp-calls).

## Quick checklist

- Use **Incognito** for things you don't want saved.
- **Delete** chats you no longer need.
- **Disconnect** apps you aren't using.
- **Check** a chat before you share a public link.
- **Push** your Project Mode work to GitHub when you're done.

## Common questions

<details><summary>Can other Classgrid AI users read my chats?</summary>Not unless you share them. Only people you invite with Share Grid, or anyone you give a share link to, can see a chat.</details>

<details><summary>Does Classgrid keep my code?</summary>Your code is kept on GitHub. Classgrid only holds a temporary copy while you work in the editor, then removes it.</details>
