Grids has extra tools that help groups decide things and make sure important messages are seen. This page covers polls and every option you can add to a message.

## Polls

Polls are the quickest way to let a group decide something, like a date, a place or a plan.

### Create a poll

1. Open the group in **Grids**.
2. Choose **Create a Poll**.
3. Type the **Question**.
4. Fill in the **Options**. Use **Add Option** to add more. A poll needs at least 2 options and can have up to 10.
5. Turn on **Allow Multiple Answers** if people may pick more than one option.
6. Send the poll.

Members tap an option to vote. Open **Poll Results** to see how many votes each option has.

<callout type="info">You can also ask the AI to create a poll for you and report the votes later. See the AI in Grids page.</callout>

## Message Options

Before you send a message, open **Message Options** in the message box. You can set three things.

| Option | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Priority** | Normal, High or Urgent. High and Urgent messages show a **High Priority** or **Urgent** badge. |
| **Auto-Delete At (Optional)** | Pick a date and time. The message deletes itself then. |
| **Schedule For (Optional)** | Pick a date and time. The message is sent then, not now. |

### Scheduled messages

Messages you schedule wait in **Scheduled Messages** until their time. Open it to check what is waiting.

<callout type="warning">Only people can schedule Grid messages. The AI cannot post into a group on a schedule.</callout>

### Example: tomorrow's reminder

1. Type "Reminder: meeting at 10 am, please bring your notes."
2. Open **Message Options**.
3. Set **Priority** to **Urgent**.
4. Set **Schedule For** to tomorrow at 8 am.
5. Send. The message goes out at 8 am with an Urgent badge.

<callout type="info">The AI chat also has its own Schedules, but those send reminders only to you, by email or WhatsApp. To send a message into a group later, use Schedule For here.</callout>

## Pin messages

Keep an important message at the top of a chat with **Pin message**. Choose how long it stays pinned:

- 24 hours
- 7 days
- 30 days

## Star messages

Star any message to save it for later. Open **Starred messages** to see everything you starred in one place.

## Disappearing messages

Turn on **Disappearing messages** in a chat to have new messages delete themselves after a set time:

- 24 hours
- 7 days
- 90 days

This is handy for casual chats where you don't need to keep the history.

## Read receipts

Want to know who has seen your message? Open **Message Info** on a message you sent. It shows:

- **Read by** — people who have opened it
- **Delivered to** — people it reached who haven't opened it yet
- **Sent** — when it left

This works well for updates in a group: post the update, then check **Read by** later.

## Other handy tools

- **Search in chat** to find an old message.
- **Mention all members in this chat** to get everyone's attention in a group.
- **Forward message to** to pass a message on to another chat.
- Reactions and replies to answer a single message.

## Common questions

<details><summary>Can people pick more than one option in a poll?</summary>Yes, if you turned on Allow Multiple Answers when you created the poll.</details>

<details><summary>Where do I see messages I scheduled?</summary>Open Scheduled Messages. It lists the messages that are waiting to send.</details>

<details><summary>What is the difference between auto-delete and disappearing messages?</summary>Auto-delete is set on one message, at an exact date and time. Disappearing messages is a chat setting that applies to all new messages in that chat after 24 hours, 7 days or 90 days.</details>
