You can shape how Classgrid AI talks to you. Set a default tone and length, tell it who you are, and add your own **skills**, which are saved instructions for a kind of job. Everything you set here is used in every chat.

## Open the Skills panel

1. In the message box, click the **sliders icon** (**AI Hub**).
2. Open the **Skills** section.

You'll find three parts: **Behavior Defaults**, **Custom Instructions** and your custom skills.

## Behavior Defaults

Behavior Defaults set the general style of every answer.

| Setting | What it changes |
|---|---|
| **Base Tone** | The overall voice of the AI |
| **Verbosity** | How long answers are |
| **Format** | How answers are laid out |
| **Emoji** | How much the AI uses emoji |
| **Explanation Level** | How simple or detailed explanations are |

Pick the options that suit you. You can change them any time.

## Custom Instructions

Custom Instructions tell the AI about you, so it doesn't have to ask every time.

| Field | What to write |
|---|---|
| **Nickname** | What you'd like the AI to call you |
| **Occupation** | Your job, for example "Shop owner" or "Freelance designer" |
| **What would you like Classgrid AI to know about you?** | Background that helps, for example your city, your work, the tools you use or your goals |
| **How would you like Classgrid AI to respond?** | Your preferences, for example "Use simple English", "Show the steps in calculations" or "Give code in TypeScript" |

<callout type="info">

Keep it short and useful. A few clear lines work better than a long essay. Don't put passwords or other secrets here.

</callout>

## Add a custom skill

A skill is a saved set of instructions for a certain kind of task. When you add one, the AI follows it in your chats.

1. In the **Skills** section, click **Add Custom Skill**.
2. Give it a clear name, for example **Meeting notes**.
3. Write the instructions. For example: *"When I share meeting notes, turn them into a short summary, a list of decisions and a table of action items with owners."*
4. Save it.

### Skill ideas

| Skill name | Instructions |
|---|---|
| **Meeting notes** | Turn my notes into a summary, decisions and action items. |
| **Short summaries** | Summarise documents as short bullet points with key terms in bold. |
| **Code reviewer** | When I paste code, list bugs first, then suggestions, then a fixed version. |
| **Caption writer** | Write social media captions in a friendly tone with 3 hashtags. |
| **Hinglish replies** | Reply in simple Hinglish unless I write in English. |

## Let the AI save a skill for you

You can also create a skill just by asking in chat. Start your message with **"remember…"** or **"from now on…"**, and the AI saves it as a skill for you. A skill card appears in the chat to show it was saved.

Examples:

- *"Remember that I run a small bakery in Pune and I'm planning a website."*
- *"From now on, always give me the answer first and the explanation after."*
- *"Remember that my company name is Sunrise Bakery and our colours are orange and cream."*

## How it all fits together

| Where | Best for |
|---|---|
| **Behavior Defaults** | The general style of every answer |
| **Custom Instructions** | Facts about you and how you like replies |
| **Custom skills** | Instructions for a specific kind of task |
| **"remember…" in chat** | Quickly saving something without opening the panel |

## FAQ

<details>
<summary>Do my settings apply to every chat?</summary>

Yes. Behavior Defaults, Custom Instructions and your skills are given to the AI with your messages, so they shape every answer.

</details>

<details>
<summary>The AI isn't following my skill. What can I do?</summary>

Make the instructions shorter and more direct, and check that your skills don't conflict with each other or with your Custom Instructions.

</details>

<details>
<summary>Is "remember…" the same as Custom Instructions?</summary>

Not quite. Saying "remember…" or "from now on…" in chat creates a skill. Custom Instructions are the fields you fill in yourself under AI Hub → Skills. Both are used in your chats.

</details>
