Verifying your WhatsApp number links it to your Classgrid AI account. It takes about a minute, and it unlocks WhatsApp calls, results and files sent to WhatsApp, and WhatsApp reminders.

## What verifying unlocks

| Feature | What you get |
|---|---|
| **WhatsApp calls** | Call Classgrid AI on WhatsApp, say what you need, and get the result after you hang up. See [WhatsApp calls](/docs/whatsapp-calls). |
| **Results on WhatsApp** | The agent can send you messages and real files (PDF, image, audio, video) on WhatsApp. See [WhatsApp messages](/docs/whatsapp-messages). |
| **Reminders on WhatsApp** | [Schedules](/docs/schedules-and-reminders) can send your reminder text to WhatsApp as well as email. |
| **Tasks that wait for you** | [Long tasks](/docs/long-tasks) can pause, wait for your WhatsApp reply, then carry on. |

## Before you start

- Sign in at [chat.classgrid.in](https://chat.classgrid.in).
- Have the phone with your WhatsApp open.
- Save **+91 81492 77038** as a contact (for example "Classgrid AI"). This is Classgrid's official WhatsApp number.

## Step by step

### 1. Say hi on WhatsApp first

Send any message (for example "Hi") to **+91 81492 77038** on WhatsApp. This lets WhatsApp deliver your login code to you.

The Classgrid help bot replies:

> "Hi! 👋 Thanks for messaging Classgrid. ✅ Done! Your WhatsApp is now ready to receive your login code."

### 2. Open the Verify WhatsApp screen

Go back to the **Verify WhatsApp** screen on chat.classgrid.in.

### 3. Enter your number

Choose your country code (India: **+91**) and enter your WhatsApp number.

### 4. Tap Send OTP

Tap **Send OTP**. Your 6-digit code arrives in the same WhatsApp chat within a few seconds.

### 5. Enter the code

Type the 6-digit code on the Verify WhatsApp screen. Your number is now verified.

<callout type="info">

The code is valid for **10 minutes**. If it does not arrive, you can ask for a new one after **60 seconds**.

</callout>

## Rules to know

- **One number, one account.** WhatsApp calls only work from a number that is verified on exactly one Classgrid AI account. If the same number is on more than one account, calls are not answered.
- **WhatsApp calls, not phone calls.** The AI answers WhatsApp voice calls only. A regular phone call will not reach it.
- **Texting does not start a task.** A new task starts from a WhatsApp call or an [email to the agent](/docs/email-the-agent). While a task is running, you can send extra text, photos or PDFs on WhatsApp and they are passed to that task.
- **Credits.** Calls and tasks use your AI credits. You can top up from **Upgrade** in the AI Hub. See [Credits and top-ups](/docs/credits-and-top-ups).

## Troubleshooting

| You see | What to do |
|---|---|
| "This WhatsApp number is not linked to a Classgrid account. Sign in on https://chat.classgrid.in and verify this number, then call again." | Your number is not verified yet. Follow the steps above, then call again. |
| The code did not arrive | Make sure you messaged +91 81492 77038 first, check you chose the right country code, then wait 60 seconds and tap **Send OTP** again. |
| The code does not work | It may be older than 10 minutes. Ask for a new one. |
| "You're out of Classgrid AI credits right now. Top up on https://chat.classgrid.in to use calls." | Top up from **Upgrade** in the AI Hub, then call again. |

## FAQ

<details>
<summary>Why do I have to message the number first?</summary>

Saying hi first gets your WhatsApp ready to receive the login code. The help bot confirms this with "✅ Done! Your WhatsApp is now ready to receive your login code."

</details>

<details>
<summary>Can I just text the number to ask the AI something?</summary>

A plain text with no task running goes to the help bot, not the agent. To start a task, [call on WhatsApp](/docs/whatsapp-calls) or [email the agent](/docs/email-the-agent). For quick questions, use the chat at chat.classgrid.in.

</details>

<details>
<summary>What language do calls use?</summary>

Calls are in English. The AI replies in English.

</details>
