Sometimes you want to ask something without it staying in your chat list. An **Incognito chat** is a private, short-lived chat. It is not saved to your history or search, and it is deleted when you close it.

## Start an Incognito chat

1. In Agent mode, choose **Incognito chat**.
2. The screen shows **"You're incognito"**.
3. Type your message as usual.

Under the heading you'll see this note:

> "Incognito chats are not saved to your history or search. They stay in this browser until you close them."

## What Incognito means

| | Normal chat | Incognito chat |
|---|---|---|
| Shows in your chat history | Yes | No |
| Shows in chat search | Yes | No |
| Stays after you close it | Yes | No, closing it deletes the chat |
| Where it lives while open | Your account | This browser only |
| Can be shared with Share Grid | Yes | No |

### It stays in this browser

While an Incognito chat is open, it lives in the browser you started it in. If you open Classgrid AI on your phone or another browser, you won't see it there.

### Closing deletes it

When you close the Incognito chat, it is deleted, including on Classgrid's side. You can't get it back later, so copy anything you want to keep before you close it.

<callout type="warning">

There is no undo. Once you close an Incognito chat, it is gone. If you might need the answer later, copy it or download any file first.

</callout>

## Good times to use Incognito

- Asking a quick one-off question you don't need in your history.
- Trying out an idea before starting a "real" chat.
- Asking something personal you'd rather not see in your chat list later.
- Using Classgrid AI on a shared or family computer, and closing the chat when you're done.

## Things to know

- **Can't share it.** Incognito chats can't be shared with Share Grid. See [Share Grid](/docs/share-grid).
- **Credits still apply.** Messages in an Incognito chat use your AI credits like any other chat. See [Credits and top-ups](/docs/credits-and-top-ups).
- **It is still your account.** You are signed in, so your normal account rules apply. Incognito only changes whether the chat is kept.
- **Want to keep it after all?** Start a normal chat instead and ask again.

For how Classgrid handles your data in general, see [Privacy and data](/docs/privacy-and-data).

## FAQ

<details>
<summary>Will an Incognito chat show up in my history later?</summary>

No. Incognito chats are not saved to your history or search.

</details>

<details>
<summary>What happens if I close the tab?</summary>

The Incognito chat stays in this browser until you close it. Closing the chat deletes it, and you can't bring it back.

</details>

<details>
<summary>Can I see my Incognito chat on another device?</summary>

No. It stays in the browser where you started it.

</details>

<details>
<summary>Can I invite friends to an Incognito chat?</summary>

No. Incognito chats can't be shared with Share Grid.

</details>
