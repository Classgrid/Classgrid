Classgrid AI can read the files you upload and work with them: summarise a long PDF, pull numbers out of a spreadsheet, describe an image or turn a voice note into text. You can attach up to 6 files to one message.

## File types it reads

| Type | Files | What the agent does |
|---|---|---|
| **PDF** | `.pdf` | Reads the text, tables and pages. |
| **Word** | `.docx` | Reads the document text. |
| **PowerPoint** | `.pptx` | Reads every slide, including speaker notes. |
| **Excel and CSV** | `.xlsx`, `.xls`, `.csv` | Reads the sheets and data. It can also clean and analyse them in the [code sandbox](/docs/code-and-data). |
| **Text** | `.txt`, `.md` | Reads the text. |
| **Images** | photos, screenshots, scans | Looks at the image and describes or reads it. |
| **Audio** | voice notes, `.mp3`, `.wav`, `.webm` | Turns speech into text. |
| **Video** | video files | Pulls out the audio and turns the speech into text. |

## How to add files

### Upload from your computer

1. Click the paperclip (**Attach file**) in the message box, or type `@` and pick **Add photos & files** ("Upload from computer").
2. Choose up to **6 files** for one message.
3. Type what you want and send.

### Reuse a file from your Library

Every file you upload, and every file the AI makes for you (PDF, Word, Excel, PowerPoint), is kept in your [Library](/docs/library). Images you create are in the AI Hub under **Images**. Type `@` and pick **Add from library** ("Browse and search your files") to attach one again without uploading it.

## Voice notes and audio

- **Dictate:** click the mic (**Dictate**) in the message box and speak. Your words are typed into the box for you to check and send.
- **Voice notes and recordings:** upload an audio file and ask, for example, "Turn this voice note into text" or "Make notes from this meeting recording."
- **Videos:** upload a video and ask "Summarise what is said in this video." For a YouTube link, just paste the link and ask for a summary.

## Images

Upload a photo, screenshot or scan and ask about it:

- "What does this error message on my screen mean?"
- "Read the text in this photo of the notice board."
- "Explain this diagram from the manual."
- "Copy the items and prices from this photo of a bill into a table."

To make a new image instead, see [Images](/docs/images).

## What you can ask

| Goal | Try asking |
|---|---|
| Notes | "Turn this PDF into clear notes." |
| Remember key points | "Make 15 flashcards from these slides." / "Create a quiz from a PDF." |
| Quick summary | "Summarise this 40-page report in one page." |
| Compare documents | "Compare these two contracts and list the differences." |
| Data | "Clean up messy spreadsheet data and show totals by month." |
| Reports | "Turn this sales sheet into a short progress report." |
| Meetings | "Turn this meeting recording into notes and action items." |
| New file | "Rewrite this as a Word document" or "Make a PDF from these notes." |

Answers can come as [answer cards](/docs/answer-cards), for example flashcards, quizzes, charts or score bars. To get a file back, see [Code and data](/docs/code-and-data).

## Tips

- **Say what you want from the file.** "Summarise" is fine, but "List every deadline and who owns it" gets a sharper answer.
- **Point to the part you need.** "Only chapter 3" or "just the sheet called Sales".
- **Attach related files together.** Up to 6 in one message, so the agent can compare or combine them.
- **Ask follow-ups.** The agent remembers the files in the chat, so you can keep asking without uploading again.
- **Check important numbers.** For money, dates or contracts, ask the agent to quote the exact line or cell it used.

<callout type="info">

Want to use a file from Google Drive, Notion or another app? Connect it as a [plugin](/docs/plugins) and ask the agent to find it, for example "Find last week's invoice in my Drive."

</callout>

## FAQ

<details>
<summary>How many files can I attach?</summary>

Up to 6 files per message. You can add more in your next message.

</details>

<details>
<summary>Can it read scanned PDFs or photos of pages?</summary>

Yes, it can look at images and scans and read the text in them. Clear, well-lit photos work best.

</details>

<details>
<summary>Where do my files go?</summary>

They are kept in your [Library](/docs/library), where you can search, filter and sort them. For private one-off chats, use [Incognito](/docs/incognito). See also [Privacy and data](/docs/privacy-and-data).

</details>
