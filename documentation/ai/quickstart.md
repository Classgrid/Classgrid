This guide takes you from a new account to your first real result in a few minutes. Each step links to a page with more detail.

## 1. Sign up

1. Go to [chat.classgrid.in](https://chat.classgrid.in).
2. Create your account and sign in.
3. You land on the Agent mode home screen, with a greeting and a message box that says "Ask anything, analyse a file, or schedule a task…".

Classgrid AI is free to start. When you need more, you can top up from **Upgrade** in the AI Hub. See [Credits and top-ups](/docs/credits-and-top-ups).

## 2. Run your first task

Type a request in plain words and press Enter. Good first tries:

- "Search the web for this week's top AI news and give me 5 points with sources."
- "Make me a 7-day plan for a family trip to Jaipur, with a day-by-day schedule and a rough budget."
- "Build me a simple, modern one-page personal portfolio website."

Watch the steps appear as the agent works: it may search, open pages, run code or build files before it answers. Answers can include [answer cards](/docs/answer-cards) like charts, planners or quizzes.

<callout type="info">

Prefer talking? Click **Dictate** (the mic) in the message box and speak. Your words are turned into text in the box.

</callout>

## 3. Upload a file

1. Click the paperclip (**Attach file**), or type `@` and pick **Add photos & files**.
2. Choose up to 6 files. PDF, Word, PowerPoint, Excel, CSV, text, images, audio and video all work.
3. Ask something about them, for example "Turn this PDF into clear notes and 10 flashcards."

Files you have uploaded before are in your [Library](/docs/library). Type `@` and pick **Add from library** to reuse one. More in [Files and documents](/docs/files-and-documents).

## 4. Connect a plugin

Plugins let the agent work with your other apps, such as Gmail, Google Calendar, Google Drive, Notion, Slack and GitHub.

1. Click the sliders icon (**AI Hub**) in the message box.
2. Open the plugins list and pick an app.
3. Connect it and sign in to that app when asked.
4. Use one of the "Try asking the AI" examples, such as "What's on my calendar for tomorrow?" or "Read my unread emails from today".

Plugins are also listed in the `@` menu. See [Plugins](/docs/plugins) and [Connect and disconnect apps](/docs/connect-and-disconnect-apps).

## 5. Verify your WhatsApp

Verifying your WhatsApp number lets you call Classgrid AI, get results and files on WhatsApp, and get reminders there.

1. Send any message to **+91 81492 77038** on WhatsApp.
2. On chat.classgrid.in, open the **Verify WhatsApp** screen.
3. Choose your country code, enter your number and tap **Send OTP**.
4. Enter the 6-digit code that arrives in WhatsApp.

Full steps in [Verify your WhatsApp](/docs/verify-whatsapp).

## 6. Try a WhatsApp call

WhatsApp-call **+91 81492 77038** from your verified number. The AI answers: "Hi {your name}, this is Classgrid AI. What can I do for you?" Say what you need in English, hang up, and the result comes back on WhatsApp. See [WhatsApp calls](/docs/whatsapp-calls).

<callout type="warning">

This is a WhatsApp voice call, not a normal phone call. A regular phone call will not reach the AI.

</callout>

## FAQ

<details>
<summary>Do I have to pick a model first?</summary>

No. The model picker starts on **Auto**, which "Picks the best model for each message". You can change it any time. See [Choose a model](/docs/choose-a-model).

</details>

<details>
<summary>Do I need to verify WhatsApp to use the chat?</summary>

No. The web chat works without it. You only need it for WhatsApp calls, WhatsApp delivery and WhatsApp reminders.

</details>

<details>
<summary>Can I chat without saving to my history?</summary>

Yes. Start an **Incognito chat**. It is not saved to your history or search, and closing it deletes it. See [Incognito](/docs/incognito).

</details>
