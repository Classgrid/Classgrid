Classgrid AI answers are not just text. When it helps, the agent replies with ready-made cards: charts, diagrams, flashcards, quizzes, planners, live weather, maps and more. You can tap, reveal, copy and interact with them right in the chat.

## How to get a card

You do not need a special command. The agent picks a card when it fits, and you can always ask for one:

- "Show this as a bar chart."
- "Draw a diagram of this process."
- "Make flashcards from this PDF."
- "Quiz me on this."
- "What's the weather in Pune this week?"

## Charts and diagrams

| Card | What it shows | Try asking |
|---|---|---|
| **Chart** | Bar, line, pie, doughnut or radar charts. | "Show monthly sales as a line chart." |
| **Diagram** | Flowcharts, timelines, mind maps and other diagrams. | "Draw a flowchart of our order process." |

Maths in answers is shown with proper formulas.

## Learning and practice

| Card | What it shows |
|---|---|
| **Flashcards and slides** | A swipeable set of cards. Flashcards keep the answer hidden until you reveal it. |
| **Quiz** | Clickable multiple-choice questions with **Next**, **Skip** and "Something else", and a score at the end. |
| **Follow-up questions** | Three suggested next questions you can tap to ask. |

Try: "Make 10 flashcards from this article" or "Quiz me on these meeting notes so I remember the key points."

## Planning

| Card | What it shows |
|---|---|
| **Planner** | A day-by-day plan with ticks and days left, and a **Set reminders** button. |
| **Countdown** | A live countdown to a date, or a timer. |
| **Timetable** | A weekly grid. |
| **Plan card** | A plan the agent wants to follow, for you to approve before it starts big work. |

Try: "Make me a 2-week plan to prepare for my product launch" or "Count down to 31 December."

<callout type="info">

**Set reminders** on a planner asks the agent to set up a [schedule](/docs/schedules-and-reminders) for the plan. Reminders send the text written when you set them up, by email and WhatsApp if you choose.

</callout>

## Live cards

| Card | What it shows | Try asking |
|---|---|---|
| **Weather** | Current weather plus the next 5 days. | "Weather in Bengaluru this week." |
| **Market** | A stock or currency price with a chart. | "Show the USD to INR rate." |
| **Map** | A pin for a place, or a route between places. | "Show the route from Pune to Lonavala." |
| **Clock** | The live time in a place. | "What time is it in London?" |

## Reviews and summaries

| Card | What it shows |
|---|---|
| **Review** | A report-style header for a summary or comparison. |
| **Scores** | Score bars, for example when comparing options. |
| **Rating** | A star-rating card. |
| **Points** | Key points as icon rows. |
| **Cards** | Rows with pictures. |

Web answers with these cards show **[n]** citation links. See [Web research](/docs/web-research).

Try: "Compare the top 3 project management apps and score each one."

## Copy-ready blocks

When the agent writes something you will send elsewhere, such as an email, a message or a prompt, it can put it in a block with one-click copy.

Try: "Write a polite email asking for an invoice update" or "Write a WhatsApp message inviting friends to dinner on Saturday."

## Action cards

**Action** cards show buttons or answers you can reveal. They are handy for step-by-step guides and quick checks.

## Mini widgets

For something interactive, the agent can build its own small card on the spot, for example:

- a poll
- a slider to try different numbers
- a loan or tip calculator
- a small game

Widgets run in a safe, separate frame inside the chat.

Try: "Make a calculator that splits a dinner bill with tip between friends."

## Skill cards

When the agent saves a skill for you (for example after you say "from now on, keep answers short"), it shows a skill card. See [Skills and custom instructions](/docs/skills-and-custom-instructions).

## Tips

- **Ask for the card by name** if you have one in mind: "as a chart", "as flashcards", "as a planner".
- **Combine them.** "Give me a 30-day fitness plan as a planner and a countdown to day 30."
- **Turn a card into a file.** "Make this chart into a PDF report" or "Put these flashcards in a PowerPoint." See [Code and data](/docs/code-and-data).
- **Share it.** Share the chat as a link so others can see the cards. See [Share links](/docs/share-links).

## FAQ

<details>
<summary>Can I download a chart?</summary>

Ask the agent to make it as an image or put it in a PDF or PowerPoint. See [Code and data](/docs/code-and-data).

</details>

<details>
<summary>Are the weather and market cards live?</summary>

Yes. They show current data at the time you ask. Ask again later for an update.

</details>

<details>
<summary>Can I answer a quiz question that has no right option?</summary>

Yes. Quiz cards have **Skip** and "Something else", so you can move on or type your own answer.

</details>
