Classgrid AI is an AI agent that does real work, not just chat. You ask in plain words at [chat.classgrid.in](https://chat.classgrid.in), and it searches the web with sources, reads your files, writes and runs code, makes PDFs and images, publishes websites, sends email and keeps working on long tasks in the background. It is built in India and works in India.

## What makes Classgrid AI different

Three things set Classgrid AI apart from a normal chatbot:

- **You can call it on WhatsApp.** Say what you need, hang up, and the finished work comes back to your WhatsApp.
- **It has Project Mode.** A real code editor in your browser, linked to GitHub, where every change the AI makes arrives as a pull request you review.
- **It has Grids.** Built-in messaging and groups, plus one AI chat shared by up to 4 people (you included).

### Agent mode

This is the home screen of chat.classgrid.in. The message box says "Ask anything, analyse a file, or schedule a task…". From here you can:

- [Search the web](/docs/web-research) and get answers with numbered **[n]** citations.
- [Upload files](/docs/files-and-documents) such as PDFs, Word, PowerPoint, Excel, images and voice notes.
- [Create images](/docs/images) from a text description.
- [Run code and work with data](/docs/code-and-data), and get Word, PowerPoint, Excel or PDF files back.
- [Publish a website](/docs/publish-a-website) at `yourname.sites.classgrid.in`.
- Get rich [answer cards](/docs/answer-cards) like charts, flashcards, quizzes and planners.
- Use [plugins](/docs/plugins) such as Gmail, Google Calendar, Notion, GitHub and Slack.
- Set [reminders](/docs/schedules-and-reminders) and run [long tasks](/docs/long-tasks).

### Project Mode

Switch to it from the sidebar with **Switch Mode** → **Project**. You need a GitHub account. The agent reads, edits and runs your code, and saves your work as pull requests. See [Project Mode](/docs/project-mode).

### Grids

Find **Grids** in the main menu. Chat with anyone on Classgrid AI, create groups and polls, and ask the AI to summarise a group or draft a post (it always asks before sending). See [Grids](/docs/grids).

### WhatsApp

Verify your WhatsApp number, then WhatsApp-call Classgrid AI on **+91 81492 77038**. See [Verify your WhatsApp](/docs/verify-whatsapp) and [WhatsApp calls](/docs/whatsapp-calls).

## FAQ

<details>
<summary>Do I need to install anything?</summary>

No. Classgrid AI runs in your browser at chat.classgrid.in. Project Mode also runs in the browser, with nothing to install.

</details>

<details>
<summary>Can I phone Classgrid AI on a normal phone line?</summary>

No. It answers **WhatsApp voice calls** only, from a WhatsApp number you have verified on your account. A regular phone call will not reach the AI.

</details>

<details>
<summary>Is my data private?</summary>

You control what you share. You can use an [Incognito chat](/docs/incognito) that is not saved to your history. Read [Privacy and data](/docs/privacy-and-data) for more.

</details>
