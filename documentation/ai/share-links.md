A share link turns one of your AI chats into a public page that anyone with the link can read. It is a good way to show someone a useful answer, a plan or a piece of work without inviting them into the chat.

## Share a chat

1. At [chat.classgrid.in](https://chat.classgrid.in), find the chat in your chat history in the sidebar.
2. Open the chat's menu and choose **Share**.
3. Pick one of the four buttons.

| Button | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Copy link** | Copies the public link so you can paste it anywhere |
| **WhatsApp** | Opens WhatsApp with the link and a ready message |
| **LinkedIn** | Opens LinkedIn with the link and a ready message |
| **Email** | Emails the chat to **yourself** |

The WhatsApp and LinkedIn buttons fill in a short message for you: "Read the chat that I recently had on my Classgrid agent…" You can change it before you post.

<callout type="info">The Email button sends the chat to your own email address, not to someone else. To send it to another person, use Copy link and paste the link in your own email.</callout>

## What people see

- The link opens a read-only page on **share.classgrid.in**.
- The page shows the chat and a **Shared by** line with your name.
- Readers can't type in the chat, ask the AI anything or change it.
- Readers don't need a Classgrid AI account to read the page.

<callout type="warning">Share links are public. Anyone who has the link can read the chat. Before you share, check that the chat has no private details, such as phone numbers, addresses, passwords or anything from your connected apps.</callout>

## Share links or Share Grid?

| | Share link | Share Grid |
|---|---|---|
| Who can see it | Anyone with the link | Only the people you invite |
| Can they send messages? | No, read only | Yes |
| Can they ask the AI? | No | Yes, one person at a time |
| Account needed? | No | Yes |
| How many people | No set number | Up to 4 people |

Use a share link to **show** a chat. Use [Share Grid](/docs/share-grid) to **work on** a chat together.

## Good uses

- Post a finished plan or research answer on LinkedIn.
- Send a friend the steps the AI gave you, over WhatsApp.
- Share a how-to guide with your community.
- Keep a copy of a chat in your own inbox with the Email button.

## Common questions

<details><summary>Do readers need to sign in?</summary>No. The shared page is public, so anyone with the link can open and read it.</details>

<details><summary>Can people reply on the shared page?</summary>No. The shared page is read only. To let people take part, invite them with Share Grid.</details>

<details><summary>Can I share an Incognito chat?</summary>Incognito chats are not saved to your history, so they don't appear in the sidebar to share.</details>
