Grids is the messaging part of Classgrid AI. You can chat one to one with anyone who has a Classgrid AI account, create groups, run polls, and share an AI chat with friends or teammates. The AI can also read and summarise your groups, and post in them after you say yes.

## Where to find Grids

1. Sign in at [chat.classgrid.in](https://chat.classgrid.in).
2. Open the main menu and choose **Grids**.

<callout type="info">If you are inside the Agent view, the sidebar shows your AI chat history. Tap Back once to see the main menu with Grids, Requests and Audit Logs.</callout>

Grids shows an unread badge when new messages arrive. New messages also pop up as small notices on any page you are on, and they appear in the bell menu. You can turn these alerts on or off with the **Grid messages** setting ("New messages in DMs and groups").

## One to one chats

To start a chat with one person:

1. In Grids, choose **New chat**.
2. Look through **Contacts on Classgrid** or search for the person.
3. Choose **Start Chat**.

Anyone with a Classgrid AI account can be found here. Grids connects Classgrid AI users only. It does not link to WhatsApp or other messaging apps.

### What you can send

- Text, voice notes and GIFs
- **Photos & Videos** and **Document** files
- @mentions, including "Mention all members in this chat" in groups
- Reactions, replies and **Forward message to**

You can also search inside a chat, star messages, pin messages, block contacts, archive chats and use **Mark all as read**. The chat list has filters for **All**, **Unread**, **Groups** and **Archived**.

### Live status

You can see when someone is **Online**, and when they are typing, recording or uploading. Open **Message Info** on any message you sent to see **Read by**, **Delivered to** and **Sent**.

## Groups

Choose **New group**, give it a **Group name** and, if you like, a **Group subject**. Then pick a group type and a privacy option. You decide who can send messages, who can add members and more.

Read the full guide: [Groups and permissions](/docs/groups-and-permissions).

## Polls and message options

Groups can run polls with 2 to 10 options. Any message can be marked **Urgent** or **High Priority**, set to delete itself at a set time, or scheduled to send later.

Read more: [Polls and message options](/docs/polls-and-message-options).

## What the AI can do with Grids

From your AI chat, you can ask the agent to:

| Task | Can it? |
|---|---|
| Summarise what a group talked about recently | Yes |
| Create a poll and tell you the results | Yes |
| Draft and post a message or announcement | Yes, after you approve |
| Send a file to a chat or group | Yes |
| Read your one to one chats | Only after asking you |
| Post into a group on a schedule | No |

Read more: [AI in Grids](/docs/ai-in-grids).

## Working on one AI chat together

Grids also covers sharing your AI chats:

- **Share Grid** lets up to 4 people use the same AI chat together. See [Share Grid](/docs/share-grid).
- **Share links** turn a chat into a public, read-only page. See [Share links](/docs/share-links).

## Common questions

<details><summary>Do the other people need a Classgrid AI account?</summary>Yes. Grids chats and groups are between Classgrid AI users. Anyone can sign up for free at chat.classgrid.in.</details>

<details><summary>Is there an AI bot inside my group chats?</summary>No. The AI does not sit inside groups or reply to @ mentions there. You ask the agent from your own AI chat, and it reads or posts for you. To use the AI together with other people, use Share Grid.</details>

<details><summary>Can I get Grid alerts while I am on another page?</summary>Yes. New messages pop up as small notices on any page, and the Grids menu item shows an unread badge.</details>
