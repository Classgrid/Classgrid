You can talk to Classgrid AI with a WhatsApp voice call. Say what you need, get quick answers on the spot, and hang up. The agent keeps working on bigger jobs after the call and sends the result to your WhatsApp.

## Before you call

1. Sign in at [chat.classgrid.in](https://chat.classgrid.in).
2. Verify your WhatsApp number. See [Verify your WhatsApp](/docs/verify-whatsapp).
3. Save **+91 81492 77038** in your contacts as "Classgrid AI".

<callout type="warning">Calls only work from a WhatsApp number that is verified on exactly one Classgrid AI account. A number shared by several accounts will not be answered.</callout>

If your number is not linked, you get this message on WhatsApp: "This WhatsApp number is not linked to a Classgrid account. Sign in on https://chat.classgrid.in and verify this number, then call again."

## Make a call

1. Open WhatsApp and find **Classgrid AI** (+91 81492 77038).
2. Tap the **voice call** button.
3. The AI answers: "Hi {your first name}, this is Classgrid AI. What can I do for you?"
4. Say what you need, in English. You can interrupt it any time.
5. Hang up when you are done.

<callout type="info">This is a WhatsApp call, not a normal phone call. Dialling the number from your phone app will not reach the AI. There is also no call button inside chat.classgrid.in.</callout>

## During the call

### Quick questions get live answers

For short things, the AI answers while you are on the line. It uses the full agent, including your connected apps such as Gmail, Google Calendar, Google Drive, Notion, GitHub and Vercel.

If an answer needs more than about 90 seconds, the AI sends it to you on WhatsApp instead, so you are not left waiting.

### Bigger jobs become tasks

For work that takes longer, like writing a report or fixing a website, the AI saves it as a task to do after you hang up. Coding work goes into [Project Mode](/docs/project-mode).

## After you hang up

1. You get a WhatsApp message: "Got it. Classgrid AI is working on it now. I'll send you the result here when it's done."
2. The agent does the work in a new chat in your account.
3. When it's finished, you get the result and a link to the chat on WhatsApp, ending with "✅ Task completed."
4. PDFs and other files come as real files you can open in WhatsApp.

If you only asked questions and no task was saved, you get a short recap titled "Your call with Classgrid AI".

If the call drops, any request you already made is saved: "Sorry, the call dropped, but your request is saved and Classgrid AI is working on it. You can call again anytime."

Tasks keep going for up to 48 hours. See [Long tasks](/docs/long-tasks).

## Add more after the call

Forgot something? While your task is running you can:

- **Send a WhatsApp message** to the same number with extra details, photos or PDFs. It replies "got it" and passes them to the task.
- **Email** [agent@classgrid.in](mailto:agent@classgrid.in) from your registered email address. See [Email the agent](/docs/email-the-agent).

## Things to know

| Rule | Details |
|---|---|
| Call length | Up to **15 minutes** per call. You get a warning near the end. |
| Language | **English only.** The AI speaks and replies in English. |
| Type of call | **WhatsApp voice call** only. Not a normal phone call. |
| Who starts the call | You. The AI never calls you or anyone else. |
| Daily limit | There is a daily limit on calls per number. If you reach it, you are told when you can call again. |
| Credits | Calls and tasks use your AI credits. See [Credits and top ups](/docs/credits-and-top-ups). |

If you are out of credits, you get: "You're out of Classgrid AI credits right now. Top up on https://chat.classgrid.in to use calls."

## Things you can say

- "Email Mr. Rao that the quote is approved." It is sent from your Gmail and confirmed on WhatsApp.
- "What's on my calendar today?"
- "Make a one page report on electric scooters in India." Hang up and get the PDF on WhatsApp.
- "Find last week's invoice in my Drive."
- "Fix the contact form on my website." It runs in Project Mode and the result comes on WhatsApp.
- "Plan a 3-day trip to Jaipur and send me the plan on WhatsApp."

## Common questions

<details><summary>Can I call in Hindi?</summary>The AI talks in English only. Please speak in English for the best results.</details>

<details><summary>Can I start a new task by sending a WhatsApp text?</summary>No. New tasks start from a call, or from an email to agent@classgrid.in. WhatsApp texts reach the agent only while a task is already running.</details>

<details><summary>Why didn't the AI pick up?</summary>Check that you are making a WhatsApp call (not a phone call), that your number is verified on chat.classgrid.in, and that you have credits left.</details>
