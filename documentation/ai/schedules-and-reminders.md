Classgrid AI can set reminders for you. Tell it what to remind you about and when, and it creates a schedule. At the set time, the reminder arrives in your email, and on WhatsApp too if your number is verified.

## How a schedule works

Read this part first, because it is the most important thing to know about schedules.

<callout type="warning">

**The reminder is written when you create the schedule.** At the set time, Classgrid sends that same pre-written email (and WhatsApp message, if you chose one). The AI does **not** run again at that time. It does not search the web, check your inbox or write a fresh report. A schedule is a reminder, not a new piece of work.

</callout>

So a schedule is perfect for "Remind me to send the weekly sales report at 6 pm every Friday". It is not the right tool for "Send me today's news every morning", because the text is fixed when you set it up.

## Create a schedule

You create schedules by asking in chat. There is no form to fill in.

1. Open a chat in Agent mode at chat.classgrid.in.
2. Say what you want and when. For example: *"Remind me every Monday, Wednesday and Friday at 7 am to go for a walk, until the end of next month."*
3. The AI writes the reminder text and creates the schedule.
4. Your schedule appears on the **Scheduled** page, with its **Repeat** label.

A few good ways to ask:

- *"Remind me tomorrow at 9 am to call the bank."*
- *"Every day at 8 pm, remind me to write down today's expenses."*
- *"Send me a WhatsApp reminder every Saturday at 10 am to pay the electricity bill."*
- *"Help me set up a daily WhatsApp reminder. Ask me what time and what it should say."*

## Repeat options

| Repeat | What it means | Example |
|---|---|---|
| **One time** | Sent once, at the date and time you choose | "Remind me on 5 November at 4 pm." |
| **Daily** | Sent every day at the same time | "Every day at 7 am." |
| **Weekly** | Sent once a week on the same day | "Every Sunday at 6 pm." |
| **Chosen days** | Sent on the weekdays you pick | "Monday, Wednesday and Friday at 7 am." |

A schedule can run for **up to 90 days**. If you need a reminder for longer than that, set it up again when it ends.

## Where reminders arrive

| Channel | When you get it |
|---|---|
| **Email** | Always. The reminder goes to the email address on your Classgrid AI account. |
| **WhatsApp** | Only if your WhatsApp number is verified and you asked for a WhatsApp reminder. See [Verify WhatsApp](/docs/verify-whatsapp). |

WhatsApp messages from Classgrid have a weekly limit. If you have used a lot of WhatsApp messages in a week, a WhatsApp reminder may not be sent. The email reminder is still sent.

## Pair reminders with a plan

Schedules work well with the AI's planners. Ask for a plan first, then ask for reminders that match it.

- *"Make me a 2-week plan to launch my landing page, with daily tasks and a review day each week."*
- Then: *"Now remind me every morning at 7 am to open my planner and start the day's task."*

Keep the reminder text general, because the same words are sent each time.

## What schedules cannot do

- They cannot run the AI again later, so they cannot send "fresh" summaries, news or reports.
- They cannot watch a website, an inbox or a price for changes.
- They cannot post into a Grid chat or group. To schedule a message in Grids, use **Schedule For** in the message options yourself. See [Polls and message options](/docs/polls-and-message-options).
- They go only to you, not to other people.

If you need the AI to actually do work at a later time, for example wait for a reply and then carry on, see [Long tasks](/docs/long-tasks).

## FAQ

<details>
<summary>Will my daily reminder have new information each day?</summary>

No. Each reminder contains the text written when the schedule was created. Nothing new is looked up when the reminder is sent.

</details>

<details>
<summary>I didn't get the WhatsApp reminder. Why?</summary>

Check that your WhatsApp number is verified on your account. WhatsApp messages also have a weekly limit. Your email reminder is still sent either way.

</details>

<details>
<summary>How far ahead can I schedule?</summary>

A schedule can cover up to 90 days.

</details>
