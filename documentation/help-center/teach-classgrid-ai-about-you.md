You can shape how Classgrid AI answers you. What you set here is used in every chat, whichever model you pick.

## Open the Skills panel

1. In the message box, click the sliders icon (**AI Hub**).
2. Open **Skills**.

![AI Hub, Skills, with Behavior Defaults, Custom Instructions and custom skills](SCREENSHOT:ai-hub-skills)

## Set your answer style

Under **Behavior Defaults**, pick options for:

- **Base Tone**: the overall voice.
- **Verbosity**: how long answers are.
- **Format**: how answers are laid out.
- **Emoji**: how much emoji to use.
- **Explanation Level**: how simple or detailed explanations are.

## Tell the AI about you

Under **Custom Instructions**, fill in:

- **Nickname**: what the AI should call you.
- **Occupation**: for example "Shop owner" or "Freelance designer".
- **What would you like Classgrid AI to know about you?**: your city, work, tools or goals.
- **How would you like Classgrid AI to respond?**: for example "Use simple English" or "Give code in TypeScript".

Keep it short. Never put passwords or other secrets here.

## Add your own skill

A skill is a saved set of instructions for one kind of job.

1. Click **Add Custom Skill**.
2. Give it a clear name, like **Meeting notes**.
3. Write the instructions: "When I share meeting notes, give me a short summary, the decisions, and a table of action items with owners."
4. Save it.

## Or just say "remember"

Start a chat message with **"remember…"** or **"from now on…"** and the AI saves it as a skill. A skill card appears in the chat. Examples:

- "Remember that I run a small bakery in Pune."
- "From now on, give me the answer first and the explanation after."

## If it doesn't work

If the AI isn't following a skill, make the instructions shorter and clearer, and check that two skills don't say opposite things.

Full details: [Skills and custom instructions](/docs/skills-and-custom-instructions).
