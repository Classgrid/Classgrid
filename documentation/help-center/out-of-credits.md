When your credits run out, the AI stops doing new work until you top up or your free use refreshes.

## What you'll see

- **In chat:** the agent can't answer until you have credits again.
- **On a WhatsApp call:** "You're out of Classgrid AI credits right now. Top up on https://chat.classgrid.in to use calls."
- **During a long task:** the task is **paused, not deleted**. You get a WhatsApp message that starts "⏸️ Your AI tokens ran out, so I paused your task…"
- **Making images** also has its own usage limit.

## Top up

1. Sign in at [chat.classgrid.in](https://chat.classgrid.in).
2. Click the sliders icon (**AI Hub**) in the message box.
3. Open **Upgrade** or **AI Credits**.
4. Choose how much to add and follow the steps to pay.

Your new credits are added to your account. A paused task can then carry on.

## Or wait

Free use refreshes after a while. The app shows the date.

## See where your credits went

Open **AI Hub** → **Usage** to see how much you've used.

![AI Hub, Usage, showing how much you've used](SCREENSHOT:ai-hub-usage)

## Use fewer credits

- Leave the model on **Auto**. Stronger models use more.
- Be clear and specific, so you need fewer back-and-forth messages.
- Put related questions in one message.
- Start a new chat for a new topic. Long chats carry more history every time.

## Before a big job

Check **AI Credits** before a long task or a WhatsApp call with lots of work, so nothing stops halfway.

## If it doesn't work

- **Topped up but still blocked?** Reload the page and check **AI Credits** again.
- **Payment went through but no credits?** Contact us from the [support page](/support).
