Every plan (Free, Pro and Max) has a limit that refills every few hours and a weekly limit. When you reach one, the AI stops doing new work until the limit comes back. On Pro and Max you can also top up to keep going.

## What you'll see

- **In chat:** a card that says "You've reached your {Plan} plan's limit. It comes back on {time}." On Free it has a **See plans** button. On Pro and Max it has a **Top up** button.
- **On a WhatsApp call:** the call is not taken. On Free: "You've used your Free plan's limit for now, so Classgrid AI can't take calls. Calls come back on {time}, or upgrade to Pro or Max for more." On Pro and Max the end says "or top up on chat.classgrid.in".
- **During a long task:** the task is **paused, not deleted**. You get a WhatsApp message: "You've used your {Plan} plan's limit for now, so I paused your task. I'll continue by myself when it comes back on {time}." It ends with the same Free or Pro and Max line as the call message.
- **Making images** also has its own limit. Pro and Max get more images.

## Wait for it to come back

Your limit comes back by itself. The message shows the time. A paused task continues by itself when it does.

## On Free: move to Pro or Max

1. Sign in at [chat.classgrid.in](https://chat.classgrid.in).
2. Open **Settings** > **Billing**, or go to [classgrid.in/pricing](https://classgrid.in/pricing).
3. Choose **Pro** or **Max** and follow the steps to pay.

## On Pro or Max: top up

1. Sign in at [chat.classgrid.in](https://chat.classgrid.in).
2. Open **Settings** > **Billing**.
3. Click **Buy credits**.
4. Choose how much to add and follow the steps to pay.

Your new credits are added to your account and are used after your plan's limit runs out. A paused task can then carry on.

## See what you have used

Open **Settings** > **Usage** to see how much you've used and when your limits come back.

![Settings, Usage, showing how much you've used](SCREENSHOT:settings-usage)

## Make your limit go further

- Leave the model on **Auto**. Stronger models use more.
- Be clear and specific, so you need fewer back-and-forth messages.
- Put related questions in one message.
- Start a new chat for a new topic. Long chats carry more history every time.

## Before a big job

Check **Settings** > **Usage** before a long task or a WhatsApp call with lots of work, so nothing stops halfway.

## If it doesn't work

- **Topped up but still blocked?** Reload the page and check **Settings** > **Usage** again.
- **Payment went through but no credits?** Contact us from the [support page](/support).
