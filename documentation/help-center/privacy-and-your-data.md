You decide what Classgrid AI keeps and what it can reach. Here are the simple controls you have.

## Your chats

- **Delete chats** you no longer need. See [Delete a chat](/help-center/article/delete-a-chat).
- **Use Incognito** for things you don't want saved. Incognito chats aren't saved to your history or search, and closing one deletes it. See [Use Incognito chats](/help-center/article/use-incognito-chats).
- Other Classgrid AI users **can't read your chats** unless you share them.

## Your connected apps

When you connect an app like Gmail or Google Drive, the agent works in it as you.

- Connect only the apps you need.
- Ask for a draft before the agent sends or posts anything.
- Disconnect apps you don't use: **AI Hub** → **Plugins** → tap the app → **Disconnect**. See [Disconnect an app](/help-center/article/disconnect-an-app).

## Your code in Project Mode

- Your code is kept on **GitHub**. Opening a project downloads it fresh.
- While you work, a temporary copy sits in your editor. It's removed after you close the editor.
- Before that, unpushed work is saved to a backup branch on GitHub, so nothing is lost.
- Deleting a project in Classgrid doesn't delete your code on GitHub.

## Sharing

- A **share link** makes a **public**, read-only page. Anyone with the link can read it. Check the chat for phone numbers, addresses, passwords or details from your apps first.
- **Share Grid** is private. Only the people you invite (up to 4 people in total) can see and use the chat.

## WhatsApp

- Calls only work from a number verified on your own account.
- Results and reminders go only to your own verified WhatsApp.

## Your files

Files you upload and files the AI makes are kept in your [Library](/help-center/article/find-files-in-library).

## Quick checklist

1. Use **Incognito** for private questions.
2. **Delete** chats you don't need.
3. **Disconnect** apps you aren't using.
4. **Check** a chat before sharing a link.
5. **Push** your Project Mode work to GitHub when you're done.

Never put passwords or secrets in your chats or in Custom Instructions.
