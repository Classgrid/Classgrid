**Grids** is the messaging part of Classgrid AI. You can chat one to one with anyone who has a Classgrid AI account, or make a group.

## Open Grids

1. Sign in at [chat.classgrid.in](https://chat.classgrid.in).
2. Open the main menu and choose **Grids**.

If you're in the Agent view, the sidebar shows your AI chat history. Tap **Back** once to see the main menu.

![The Grids screen with the chat list and an open chat](SCREENSHOT:grids)

## Start a one to one chat

1. In Grids, choose **New chat**.
2. Look through **Contacts on Classgrid** or search for the person.
3. Choose **Start Chat**.

You can send text, voice notes, GIFs, **Photos & Videos** and **Document** files. You can also react, reply and forward messages.

## Create a group

1. In Grids, choose **New group**.
2. Type a **Group name**. A **Group subject** is optional.
3. Pick a group type from the list, such as **General**.
4. Pick who can find and join it:
   - **Private (Hidden, Invite Only)**: only people you add.
   - **Approval Required (Visible, Request to Join)**: people ask to join and are approved.
   - **Public / Open (Visible, Instant Join)**: anyone on Classgrid AI can join right away.
5. Create the group, then use **Add member** to bring people in.

## Choose who can post

In the group settings, you decide who can do what with **Who can send messages?**, **Who can edit group info?**, **Who can add members?** and **Who can send attachments (Images, PDFs, etc)?**

## Good to know

- Grids connects Classgrid AI users only. It doesn't link to WhatsApp or other apps.
- Use **Explore Groups** to find groups you can see, and **Requests** to see your requests to join.
- Turn new-message alerts on or off with the **Grid messages** setting.

## If it doesn't work

**Can't find someone?** They need a Classgrid AI account. Ask them to sign up free at chat.classgrid.in.
