Classgrid AI runs in your web browser, so there is nothing to install. You can start for free.

## Steps

1. Go to [chat.classgrid.in](https://chat.classgrid.in).
2. Create your account and sign in.
3. You land on the **Agent mode** home screen. You'll see a greeting and a message box that says "Ask anything, analyse a file, or schedule a task…".

![The Agent mode home screen with the greeting and the message box](SCREENSHOT:home)

That's it. You can type your first request right away.

## It's free to start

You don't need to pay anything to try Classgrid AI. Using the AI uses credits. When you need more, you can top up from **Upgrade** in the **AI Hub** (the sliders icon in the message box). See [Credits and top-ups](/help-center/article/credits-and-top-ups).

## Use an email you check

The email on your account matters. It is where:

- Your [reminders](/help-center/article/set-a-reminder) are sent.
- [Share Grid](/help-center/article/share-an-ai-chat) invites for you arrive.
- You send tasks from when you [email the agent](/help-center/article/email-a-task-to-the-agent) at agent@classgrid.in.

## What to do next

1. [Start your first chat](/help-center/article/start-your-first-chat).
2. [Verify your WhatsApp number](/help-center/article/verify-your-whatsapp-number). You only need this for WhatsApp calls, results on WhatsApp and WhatsApp reminders. The web chat works without it.
3. [Choose a model](/help-center/article/choose-a-model), or just leave it on **Auto**.

## If it doesn't work

- Check that you typed your email correctly.
- Reload the page, or open chat.classgrid.in in another browser.
- Still stuck? Contact us from the [support page](/support).

For the full walk-through, read the [Quickstart](/docs/quickstart).
