Classgrid AI runs in your web browser, so there is nothing to install. It's free to start. Signing up takes a few short steps, and the app guides you through each one.

## Step 1: Sign up with your email

1. Go to [chat.classgrid.in](https://chat.classgrid.in).
2. Under the sign-in box, next to "Don't have an account?", click **Sign up**. The screen changes to **Create your account**.
3. Type your **first name**, **last name** and **email**, then click **Continue**.

![The Create your account screen with first name, last name and email](SCREENSHOT:onboarding-signup)

4. Check your email. Type the **6-digit code** we sent you and click **Sign In**.

![The 6-digit email code screen](SCREENSHOT:onboarding-email-code)

## Step 2: Set a password

1. You'll see **OTP Verified Successfully**. Click **Setup Password**.

![OTP Verified Successfully with the Setup Password button](SCREENSHOT:onboarding-email-verified)

2. Type a **New Password** and type it again under **Confirm Password**. It needs 8 to 64 characters, with uppercase and lowercase letters, at least 1 number and at least 1 special character.
3. Click **Save Password**. From now on you sign in with your email and this password.

![The New Password and Confirm Password screen with the password rules](SCREENSHOT:onboarding-password)

## Step 3: Verify your WhatsApp

Every account needs a verified WhatsApp number. It keeps each account real (one number per account) and lets the agent send you reminders and results.

1. On **Verify WhatsApp**, first send **Hi** to the Classgrid number, **+91 81492 77038**, on WhatsApp. You can click **Click Here to Open WhatsApp** to do it in one tap. This opens the chat so your code can reach you.
2. Back on chat.classgrid.in, pick your country code (India: +91), type your **WhatsApp Number** and click **Send OTP**.

![The Verify WhatsApp screen with the two steps and the Send OTP button](SCREENSHOT:onboarding-whatsapp)

3. A 6-digit code arrives in WhatsApp. Type it in and click **Verify OTP**.

![The WhatsApp code screen with the Verify OTP button](SCREENSHOT:onboarding-whatsapp-code)

Code not arriving? See [WhatsApp code not received](/help-center/article/whatsapp-code-not-received).

## Step 4: A little about you

1. On **Basic Information**, enter **Your Age** and click **Continue**. You need to be at least 13.

![The Basic Information screen asking for your age](SCREENSHOT:onboarding-age)

2. On **What kind of work do you do?**, pick your role from the list and click **Continue**. This helps Classgrid tailor your experience.

![The What kind of work do you do? screen with the role list](SCREENSHOT:onboarding-role)

## You're in

You'll see **Welcome to Classgrid Agent**. Click **Start Chatting**.

![The Welcome to Classgrid Agent screen with the Start Chatting button](SCREENSHOT:onboarding-welcome)

You land on the **Agent mode** home screen, with a greeting and a message box that says "Ask anything, analyse a file, or schedule a task…".

![The Agent mode home screen with the greeting and the message box](SCREENSHOT:home)

Type your first request right away. See [Start your first chat](/help-center/article/start-your-first-chat).

## Stopped halfway?

Your password is saved as soon as you set it. If you close the page before verifying WhatsApp, just sign in again with your email and password, and the app takes you back to the WhatsApp step.

## It's free to start

You don't need to pay anything to try Classgrid AI. Using the AI uses credits. When you need more, top up from **Upgrade** in the **AI Hub** (the sliders icon in the message box). See [Credits and top-ups](/help-center/article/credits-and-top-ups).

## Use an email you check

The email on your account is where your [reminders](/help-center/article/set-a-reminder) are sent, where [Share Grid](/help-center/article/share-an-ai-chat) invites arrive, and the address you [email tasks](/help-center/article/email-a-task-to-the-agent) from to agent@classgrid.in.

## If it doesn't work

- Check that you typed your email and WhatsApp number correctly.
- Didn't get the email code? Wait a minute and click **Resend code**, and check your spam folder.
- Reload the page, or open chat.classgrid.in in another browser.
- Still stuck? Contact us from the [support page](/support).
