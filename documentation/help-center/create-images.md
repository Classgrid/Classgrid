Classgrid AI can make a new image from your words, like a poster, a thumbnail or an illustration for a report.

## Steps

1. Type `@` in the message box.
2. Pick **Create image** ("Visualize anything").
3. Describe the image and send.

You can also just ask in plain words, for example "Create an image of a cosy reading corner with plants and warm evening light."

The image appears in the chat. You can download it from there.

## Find your images later

Images you create are **not** in the Library. They are kept in the **AI Hub**:

1. Click the sliders icon (**AI Hub**) in the message box.
2. Open **Images** ("My Generated Images").
3. View or download any image you made in any chat.

## Tips for better images

- **Subject first:** "A golden retriever wearing a raincoat".
- **Then the setting:** "on a rainy Mumbai street at night".
- **Pick a style:** "flat illustration", "watercolour", "photo-realistic", "3D render".
- **Say the mood and colours:** "warm and cosy", "bright pastel colours".
- **Say how you'll use it:** "wide banner", "square social post", "leave space at the top for a title".
- **Ask for "no text".** AI images often get words wrong. Add your title later, or ask the agent to put text on top of the image.

## Change an image

Ask for a new version and describe the change, for example "Same idea, but at sunset and with fewer people." To add a title, logo or border, ask: "Put the text 'Spectrum 2026' at the top of this image in white."

## Use your image

- "Send this image to my WhatsApp" (needs a [verified number](/help-center/article/verify-your-whatsapp-number)).
- "Put this image on the cover of a PDF report."
- "Use this image as the main banner on my site."

## If it doesn't work

Making images uses your credits and has its own usage limit. If you run out, see [Out of credits](/help-center/article/out-of-credits).
