An **Incognito chat** is a private, short-lived chat. It isn't saved to your history or search, and closing it deletes it.

## Steps

1. In Agent mode, choose **Incognito chat**.
2. The screen shows **"You're incognito"**.
3. Type your message as usual.

![The Incognito chat screen showing "You're incognito"](SCREENSHOT:incognito)

Under the heading you'll see: "Incognito chats are not saved to your history or search. They stay in this browser until you close them."

## What changes

| | Normal chat | Incognito chat |
|---|---|---|
| In your chat history | Yes | No |
| In chat search | Yes | No |
| Kept after you close it | Yes | No, closing deletes it |
| Seen on your other devices | Yes | No, only in this browser |
| Can use Share Grid | Yes | No |

## Before you close it

There is no undo. When you close an Incognito chat, it is deleted, including on Classgrid's side. **Copy any answer or download any file you want to keep first.**

## Good times to use it

- A quick one-off question you don't need later.
- Trying out an idea before a "real" chat.
- Something personal you'd rather not see in your chat list.
- Using a shared or family computer.

## Good to know

- Incognito messages still use your credits.
- You are still signed in, so your normal account rules apply.
- Want to keep the chat after all? Start a normal chat and ask again.

## If it doesn't work

- **Can't find your Incognito chat on your phone?** It only lives in the browser where you started it.
- **Can't share it?** Incognito chats can't be shared with Share Grid or a share link.

Full details: [Incognito](/docs/incognito).
