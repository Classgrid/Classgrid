Polls are the quickest way for a group to decide something, like a date, a place or a plan.

## Create a poll yourself

1. Open the group in **Grids**.
2. Choose **Create a Poll**.
3. Type the **Question**.
4. Fill in the **Options**. Use **Add Option** for more. A poll needs at least **2** options and can have up to **10**.
5. Turn on **Allow Multiple Answers** if people may pick more than one.
6. Send the poll.

Members tap an option to vote. Open **Poll Results** to see the votes for each option.

## Ask the AI to make a poll

You can also ask from your own AI chat in Agent mode:

1. Ask: "Make a poll in the Friends Trip group: which weekend works, 8th, 15th or 22nd? Allow multiple answers."
2. Check the poll the agent suggests and confirm.
3. Later, ask: "What are the results of the weekend poll?"
4. Ask: "Post the winning weekend in the group and ask everyone to confirm by tonight."
5. Read the draft. The agent asks "Should I send this to the group?" Say yes to send.

The agent never posts in a group until you say yes.

## Other handy message tools

Before you send any message, open **Message Options** to set:

- **Priority**: Normal, High or Urgent. High and Urgent show a badge.
- **Auto-Delete At (Optional)**: the message deletes itself at that time.
- **Schedule For (Optional)**: the message is sent later. Check waiting messages in **Scheduled Messages**.

To see who saw your message, open **Message Info** for **Read by**, **Delivered to** and **Sent**.

## If it doesn't work

- **Can't add an 11th option?** Polls have a maximum of 10 options.
- **Members can't pick two options?** **Allow Multiple Answers** must be turned on when you create the poll.
- **Want the AI to post a poll every week?** The AI can't post into Grids on a schedule. Use **Schedule For** yourself for later messages.

Full details: [Polls and message options](/docs/polls-and-message-options) and [AI in Grids](/docs/ai-in-grids).
