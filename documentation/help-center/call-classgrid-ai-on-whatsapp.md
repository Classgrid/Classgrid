You can talk to Classgrid AI with a **WhatsApp voice call**. Ask quick questions on the line, or hand over a bigger job and get the result on WhatsApp after you hang up.

## Before you call

1. [Verify your WhatsApp number](/help-center/article/verify-your-whatsapp-number) on chat.classgrid.in.
2. Save **+91 81492 77038** as a contact, for example "Classgrid AI".

## Steps

1. Open WhatsApp and find **Classgrid AI** (+91 81492 77038).
2. Tap the WhatsApp **voice call** button.
3. The AI answers: "Hi {your first name}, this is Classgrid AI. What can I do for you?"
4. Say what you need, in **English**. You can interrupt it any time.
5. Hang up when you're done.

This is a WhatsApp call, not a normal phone call. Dialling the number from your phone app won't reach the AI. There's no call button inside chat.classgrid.in.

## What happens on the call

- **Quick questions** are answered live. The AI can use your connected apps, like Gmail, Google Calendar, Google Drive, Notion, GitHub and Vercel.
- If an answer takes more than about 90 seconds, it's sent to you on WhatsApp instead.
- **Bigger jobs**, like writing a report, are saved as a task for after the call. Coding work goes into Project Mode.

## After you hang up

1. You get: "Got it. Classgrid AI is working on it now. I'll send you the result here when it's done."
2. The agent works in a new chat in your account, for up to **48 hours**.
3. You get the result and a chat link, ending with "✅ Task completed." PDFs come as real files.

If you only asked questions, you get a short recap called "Your call with Classgrid AI". If the call drops, your request is saved.

## Things you can say

- "What's on my calendar today?"
- "Make a one page report on electric scooters in India." Hang up and get the PDF.
- "Plan a 3-day trip to Jaipur and send me the plan on WhatsApp."

## Good to know

- Calls last up to **15 minutes**. You get a warning near the end.
- There's a daily limit on calls per number. If you reach it, you're told when you can call again.
- Calls and tasks use your credits.
