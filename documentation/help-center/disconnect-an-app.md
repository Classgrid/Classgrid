You can disconnect any app at any time. After that, the agent can no longer use it.

## Steps

1. In the message box, click the sliders icon (**AI Hub**).
2. Open **Plugins**.
3. Tap the connected app.
4. Tap **Disconnect**.

![AI Hub, Plugins, with a connected app open](SCREENSHOT:ai-hub-plugins)

## What happens after you disconnect

- The agent can't read, search or send anything in that app anymore.
- Things the agent already did stay in the app. For example, emails it sent stay in your Sent folder, and Drive files it uploaded stay in your Drive.
- You can connect the app again any time. See [Connect an app](/help-center/article/connect-an-app).

## Remove access from the app's side too

If you want to be extra sure, you can also remove Classgrid's access from the app's own settings, for example your Google account's security settings.

## When to disconnect

- You connected an app for one job and the job is done.
- You don't use that app with Classgrid AI anymore.
- The app isn't working and you want to connect it fresh.

## Privacy tips

- **Connect only what you need.** Fewer apps means less the agent can reach.
- **Be clear.** "Send this email to riya@example.com" is safer than "reply to everyone".
- **Ask for a draft first** before anything is sent or posted.
- **Check before you share.** If a chat shows details from your apps, don't share it with a public link.

## If it doesn't work

- **The app still looks connected?** Reload the page and open AI Hub again.
- **Want to be sure access is gone?** Also remove Classgrid from the app's own settings, as shown above.
- Still stuck? Contact us from the [support page](/support).

Full details: [Connect and disconnect apps](/docs/connect-and-disconnect-apps).
