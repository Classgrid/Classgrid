The Classgrid AI agent can work with your Grids groups. You ask from your own AI chat, and it reads, drafts and posts for you. It always checks with you before it sends anything.

## Steps

1. Open a chat in **Agent mode** at [chat.classgrid.in](https://chat.classgrid.in).
2. Ask about your group in plain words. For example:
   - "Catch me up on the project team group."
   - "Summarise what the project group agreed tonight."
   - "Draft an announcement for the community group: Sunday's meetup moves to 5 pm."
   - "Send the latest proposal PDF to the project group."
3. If the agent wants to post, it shows you a draft and asks **"Should I send this to the group?"**
4. Ask for changes if you like, then say yes to send.

The agent understands requests about groups in English, Hindi and Hinglish.

## What the agent can do

- Summarise recent messages in a group.
- Create a poll and tell you the results. See [Create a poll](/help-center/article/create-a-poll).
- Draft and post a message, after you approve.
- Send a file to a chat or group.
- List your chats and groups and find people.

## Privacy

- "Read my Grid" means your **groups** only.
- The agent **asks you first** before it reads any one to one chat.

## What it can't do

- **Old history.** It reads recent group messages, roughly the last 1 to 2 hours. Ask soon after a busy chat.
- **Post on a schedule.** "Post this every Monday" isn't possible. Use **Schedule For** in **Message Options** yourself.
- **Answer inside a group.** There's no AI bot in groups, and it doesn't reply to @ mentions there.

## Want to use the AI together?

If several people want to ask the same AI, use [Share Grid](/help-center/article/share-an-ai-chat).

## If it doesn't work

- **Summary missed earlier messages?** It only reads recent ones. Scroll the group yourself for older messages.
- **Nothing was posted?** The agent waits for your yes. Reply "yes, send it".
