Classgrid AI can send answers, PDFs, images, audio and video to your WhatsApp. They come from **+91 81492 77038** and go only to your own verified number.

## Before you start

[Verify your WhatsApp number](/help-center/article/verify-your-whatsapp-number). Results can't be sent to an unverified number.

## Get a result from a web chat

1. Open any chat at chat.classgrid.in.
2. Ask for the work and say where to send it. For example:
   - "Make a one page summary of this PDF and send it to my WhatsApp."
   - "When the report is ready, send it to me on WhatsApp."
   - "Send this image to my WhatsApp."
3. Check WhatsApp. PDFs, images, audio and video arrive as **real files** you can open and forward.

## Get a result from a call or email

Tasks started from a [WhatsApp call](/help-center/article/call-classgrid-ai-on-whatsapp) or an [email to the agent](/help-center/article/email-a-task-to-the-agent) send their result to WhatsApp by themselves. The final message has the result, a link to the chat, and ends with "✅ Task completed."

## Add more while a task is running

Forgot something? While a task is still running:

1. Send a WhatsApp message to **+91 81492 77038** with extra details, photos or PDFs.
2. You get a short "got it" reply.
3. The details are passed to the running task.

Some tasks also stop and ask you a question on WhatsApp. Just reply, and the task carries on.

## Texting alone doesn't start a task

If **no** task is running, a WhatsApp text goes to the help bot, not the agent. To start a new task, make a WhatsApp call, or email agent@classgrid.in from your account email.

## If it doesn't work

- **Nothing arrived?** Check that your number is verified.
- **Got a help bot reply instead?** No task was running. Call or email to start one.
- **Some messages stopped coming?** WhatsApp messages from Classgrid have a weekly limit.
- **Out of credits?** A running task pauses. See [Out of credits](/help-center/article/out-of-credits).

Full details: [WhatsApp messages](/docs/whatsapp-messages) and [Long tasks](/docs/long-tasks).
