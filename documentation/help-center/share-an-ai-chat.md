**Share Grid** turns a normal AI chat into a shared one. Up to **4 people**, counting you, can read the chat, send messages and ask the AI together.

## Open Share Grid

1. Open the AI chat you want to share.
2. In the chat header, tap the **Share Grid** icon. (It's an icon with no text.)
3. The **Invite people** panel opens.

![The Share Grid Invite people panel showing People 2 of 4](SCREENSHOT:share-grid)

## Invite people

Pick one way:

- **By email:** type one or more emails, like `name@gmail.com`, and choose **Invite**. They get an email with an **Accept invite** button. They must sign in with that same email.
- **From Grids:** choose **Add from Grids**, then **Search people** and select them.
- **With a link:** choose **Copy invite link** and send it, for example on WhatsApp. Choose **Turn off invite link** when you don't want more people to join with it.

The panel shows **People · N of 4**. **History** shows each invite: Pending, Accepted, Declined or Turned off.

## What the other person does

1. Opens the invite: "You're invited to a Classgrid AI chat".
2. Chooses **Log in to join** if not signed in. They need a Classgrid AI account.
3. Chooses **Join chat**, then **Open chat** to see the whole conversation.

## Working together

- Every message shows who sent it, and the AI knows who asked what.
- **One person at a time.** While the AI answers someone, others see "Agent is responding to X. You can send when it's done."
- Shared chats have a star in your chat history.
- Anyone can choose **Leave chat**. If the owner leaves, the next member becomes the owner.

## If it doesn't work

- **No Share Grid icon?** Incognito chats and Project Mode chats can't be shared.
- **Your friend can't open an emailed invite?** It works only for the email it was sent to. They must sign in with that email, or you can send them the invite link instead.
- **Need more than 4 people?** Make a group in Grids instead.
- **Just want people to read it?** Use a [share link](/help-center/article/share-a-chat-link).
