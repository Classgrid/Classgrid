A share link turns one of your AI chats into a public page that anyone with the link can read. It's a good way to show someone an answer or a plan without inviting them into the chat.

## Steps

1. At [chat.classgrid.in](https://chat.classgrid.in), find the chat in your chat history in the sidebar.
2. Open the chat's menu and choose **Share**.
3. Pick a button:

| Button | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Copy link** | Copies the public link to paste anywhere |
| **WhatsApp** | Opens WhatsApp with the link and a ready message |
| **LinkedIn** | Opens LinkedIn with the link and a ready message |
| **Email** | Emails the chat to **yourself** |

The ready message says "Read the chat that I recently had on my Classgrid agent…". You can change it before you post.

## What people see

- A read-only page on **share.classgrid.in** with a **Shared by** line showing your name.
- They can't type in the chat or ask the AI anything.
- They don't need a Classgrid AI account.

## Check before you share

**Share links are public.** Anyone with the link can read the chat. Make sure it has no phone numbers, addresses, passwords, or emails and files from your connected apps.

## Share link or Share Grid?

| | Share link | Share Grid |
|---|---|---|
| Who sees it | Anyone with the link | Only people you invite |
| Can they write? | No, read only | Yes |
| Account needed? | No | Yes |
| How many | No set number | Up to 4 people |

Use a share link to **show** a chat. Use [Share Grid](/help-center/article/share-an-ai-chat) to **work on** it together.

## If it doesn't work

- **The Email button didn't reach my friend?** It only sends to your own email. Use **Copy link** and paste it into your own email instead.
- **Can't find an Incognito chat to share?** Incognito chats aren't saved to your history, so they can't be shared.

Full details: [Share links](/docs/share-links).
