Classgrid AI can send you reminders by email, and on WhatsApp too if your number is verified. You set them by asking in chat. There is no form to fill in.

## Read this first

The reminder text is written **when you create the schedule**. At the set time, Classgrid sends that same text. The AI does **not** run again. It won't search the web, check your inbox or write anything new. A schedule is a reminder, not a new piece of work.

So "Remind me to send the weekly sales report every Friday at 6 pm" works well. "Send me today's news every morning" does not.

## Steps

1. Open a chat in Agent mode.
2. Say what you want and when. For example: "Remind me every Monday, Wednesday and Friday at 7 am to go for a walk."
3. The AI writes the reminder text and creates the schedule.
4. Your reminder shows on the **Scheduled** page with its **Repeat** label.

![The Scheduled page listing reminders with their Repeat labels](SCREENSHOT:schedule)

## Repeat options

| Repeat | Example |
|---|---|
| **One time** | "Remind me tomorrow at 9 am to call the bank." |
| **Daily** | "Every day at 8 pm, remind me to note today's expenses." |
| **Weekly** | "Every Sunday at 6 pm." |
| **Chosen days** | "Monday, Wednesday and Friday at 7 am." |

A schedule can run for **up to 90 days**. Set it up again when it ends.

## Where reminders arrive

- **Email:** always, to the email on your account.
- **WhatsApp:** only if your number is [verified](/help-center/article/verify-your-whatsapp-number) and you asked for a WhatsApp reminder. Example: "Send me a WhatsApp reminder every Saturday at 10 am to pay the electricity bill."

## What reminders can't do

- Send fresh news, summaries or reports.
- Watch a website, inbox or price for changes.
- Post into a Grids chat or group. For that, use **Schedule For** in **Message Options** yourself.
- Go to other people. Reminders go only to you.

## If it doesn't work

- **No WhatsApp reminder?** Check that your number is verified. WhatsApp messages from Classgrid also have a weekly limit. Your email reminder is still sent.
- **No email?** Check your spam folder.
