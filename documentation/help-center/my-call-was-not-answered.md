If Classgrid AI didn't pick up your call, check these things in order.

## 1. Is it a WhatsApp voice call?

The AI answers **WhatsApp voice calls** only. A normal phone call from your phone app won't reach it. Open WhatsApp, find **+91 81492 77038**, and tap the voice call button.

## 2. Is your number verified?

Your WhatsApp number must be verified on chat.classgrid.in. If it isn't, you get this WhatsApp message:

"This WhatsApp number is not linked to a Classgrid account. Sign in on https://chat.classgrid.in and verify this number, then call again."

Fix: [Verify your WhatsApp number](/help-center/article/verify-your-whatsapp-number), then call again.

## 3. Is the number on only one account?

Calls only work from a number verified on **exactly one** Classgrid AI account. If the same number is on two accounts, calls aren't answered. Keep the number on one account only.

## 4. Do you have credits?

If you're out of credits, you get:

"You're out of Classgrid AI credits right now. Top up on https://chat.classgrid.in to use calls."

Fix: top up from **Upgrade** in the **AI Hub**. See [Out of credits](/help-center/article/out-of-credits).

## 5. Did you reach the daily call limit?

There's a daily limit on calls per number. If you reach it, you're told when you can call again.

## Other things to know

- **Speak English.** The AI talks and replies in English.
- **Calls last up to 15 minutes.** You get a warning near the end.
- **The AI never calls you.** You always start the call.
- **Call dropped?** Any request you already made is saved and the agent keeps working on it. You can call again any time.
- If you hear "Classgrid AI can't take calls for your account right now. Please try again later.", wait a little and try again.
