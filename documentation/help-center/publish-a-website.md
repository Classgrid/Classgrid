Classgrid AI can build a website and put it live in one chat. Your site goes live at `yourname.sites.classgrid.in`.

## Steps

1. Open a chat in **Agent mode**.
2. Describe the site. For example: "Build me a simple, modern one-page personal portfolio website."
3. The agent writes the pages and styles.
4. Tell it the name you want: "Publish it as priya-designs."
5. The agent publishes it and replies with the address, like "Live at https://priya-designs.sites.classgrid.in".
6. Click the link to open it, then share it with anyone.

## Pick a good name

- **First to publish owns the name.** If a name is taken, add a word or your city.
- Lowercase letters, numbers and dashes are easiest, like `crumb-bakery`.

## Change your site

Keep chatting in the **same chat**:

- "Make the header dark blue and the buttons orange."
- "Add a section with three customer reviews."
- "Use this photo as the main banner." (attach the photo)
- "Make it look better on phones."

Then ask the agent to publish again to the same name.

## Tips

- **Give real content.** Paste your bio, services, menu or opening hours.
- **Add images.** Upload your own photos, or ask the agent to [create images](/help-center/article/create-images).
- **Check it on your phone** and ask for fixes.

**Anything you publish is public.** Don't put passwords, ID numbers or home addresses on your site.

## If it doesn't work

- **Publishing isn't offered?** Publishing to `sites.classgrid.in` works only in Agent mode chats at chat.classgrid.in. It doesn't work in Project Mode chats, or in tasks started from a WhatsApp call or an email.
- **Working in Project Mode?** Use the Vercel plugin instead. See [Deploy to Vercel](/help-center/article/deploy-to-vercel).
- **Name already taken?** Pick another name.
