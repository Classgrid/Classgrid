# Compare page: web research (checked 2026-10-28)

Official pages first, then press. "Accessed" means the page had no date, so the check date is given.

## 1. OpenAI Dots (+ 1-800-ChatGPT, ChatGPT on WhatsApp)

- **What it is:** always-on agents, each with its own cloud computer, running on GPT-6 Astra and connecting to 4,000+ apps through plugins. Source: [openai.com, Introducing dots](https://openai.com/index/introducing-dots/) (29 Sep 2026) and [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/09/29/openai-launches-dots-its-bubbly-agentic-avatar/) (29 Sep 2026).
- **Launch:** DevDay, 29 Sep 2026. Source: [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/09/29/openai-launches-dots-its-bubbly-agentic-avatar/).
- **Where you use it:** ChatGPT (mobile app, desktop app, desktop browser), Slack (DMs and mentions), Microsoft Teams (DMs). "Texting is coming soon." Source: [learn.chatgpt.com, Message your dot](https://learn.chatgpt.com/docs/dots/channels) (accessed 2026-10-28). Third-party sources say texting is a US-only iMessage/RCS beta waitlist for Pro users ([pasqualepillitteri.it](https://pasqualepillitteri.it/en/news/19302/openai-dots-personal-ai-agent-devday-2026)). **No WhatsApp** in any official channel list.
- **Calling:** **You can voice-call your dot inside ChatGPT.** You press the phone button in the dot's chat ("Select the phone button in your dot's conversation to start a call"). This is an in-app call, not a phone number. "Calls initiated by your dot are planned for after launch." Sources: [learn.chatgpt.com/docs/dots](https://learn.chatgpt.com/docs/dots) and [channels](https://learn.chatgpt.com/docs/dots/channels) (accessed 2026-10-28).
- **Plans (names only):** Pro (Pro 100 / 200 / 500), Business Premium, and Enterprise (beta, admin turns it on). Source: [learn.chatgpt.com/docs/dots](https://learn.chatgpt.com/docs/dots) and [help.openai.com, Getting started with your dot](https://help.openai.com/en/articles/20001530-getting-started-with-your-dot) (accessed 2026-10-28).
- **Countries:** Pro users 18+ get it **everywhere except the EEA, Switzerland and the UK**. Business Premium gets it in all supported ChatGPT regions. So **India is eligible**, with a gradual rollout. Same sources as above. There is no India-specific announcement.
- **1-800-ChatGPT:** still listed by OpenAI as an "experimental" feature. You can call it in the **US and Canada only**, for 30 free minutes a month, with no account needed. Source: [help.openai.com, 1-800-ChatGPT](https://help.openai.com/en/articles/10193193-1-800-chatgpt-calling-and-messaging-chatgpt-with-your-phone) (accessed 2026-10-28). The launch was 18 Dec 2024, when the free allowance was 15 minutes ([Axios](https://www.axios.com/2024/12/18/chatgpt-phone-call-whatsapp-message)).
- **ChatGPT on WhatsApp:** stopped worldwide on 15 Jan 2026 ([openai.com, transition note](https://openai.com/index/chatgpt-whatsapp-transition/)). It came back on 13 Jul 2026 in the **EEA only**, on +1-800-242-8478, with text, images and voice notes and **no calls** ([help.openai.com](https://help.openai.com/en/articles/10193193-1-800-chatgpt-calling-and-messaging-chatgpt-with-your-phone); [The Decoder](https://the-decoder.com/chatgpt-returns-to-whatsapp-in-europe-after-eu-forces-meta-to-open-the-door-to-rival-ai-bots/), 14 Jul 2026). **Not in India.**

## 2. Grok Bot (xAI)

- **What it is:** "AI teammates" with their own cloud computer (browser, files, terminal) that sign into your tools and work 24/7. Launched in beta on 11 Aug 2026. Source: [x.ai, Introducing Grok Bot](https://x.ai/news/introducing-grok-bot) (11 Aug 2026).
- **Apps:** **macOS, Windows, Linux, iOS and Android.** It can also be reached through X Chat, by tagging @bot on X (not in every region yet), and as a shared Bot in Slack. Sources: [docs.x.ai overview](https://docs.x.ai/grok-bot/overview) and [FAQ](https://docs.x.ai/grok-bot/faq) (accessed 2026-10-28). This is wider than the "Mac, Windows, iPhone" we say on our page.
- **Connected apps:** Gmail, Google Calendar, Drive, Notion, Slack, HubSpot, Amazon and more. It also works on websites that have no API. Source: [docs.x.ai connectors](https://docs.x.ai/grok/connectors/gmail-google-calendar) and [x.ai](https://x.ai/news/introducing-grok-bot).
- **Schedules:** "routines" run on a schedule, at least 5 minutes apart, with up to 50 per Bot. Source: [docs.x.ai, Skills and routines](https://docs.x.ai/grok-bot/skills-routines-and-automations) (accessed 2026-10-28).
- **Calls it makes:** Musk posted that Grok Bot "can now call someone or a business for you, make a request on your behalf, take notes on the response and send you a note summarizing the conversation." Source: [x.com/elonmusk](https://x.com/elonmusk/status/2114365361068138990) (24–25 Oct 2026, depending on time zone) and [Tesla North](https://teslanorth.com/2026/10/25/grok-bot-phone-calls-elon-musk/) (25 Oct 2026). Plans, countries and caller ID are **not disclosed**. It runs on Grok Voice 3.0, made public on 14 Oct 2026 (third-party: [FourWeekMBA](https://fourweekmba.com/ai-agents-now-make-your-calls-grok-bot-joins-metas-muse/)). No TechCrunch, Verge or Reuters story found.
- **Calling Grok Bot yourself:** no official phone number or inbound call feature found in xAI's docs.
- **WhatsApp / Telegram:** there is no official Grok WhatsApp number (third-party only: [Blueticks](https://blueticks.co/blog/grok-whatsapp), 27 Sep 2026). Grok, the chatbot and not Grok Bot, is built into Telegram through a deal xAI announced ([TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2025/05/28/xai-to-invest-300m-in-telegram-integrate-grok-into-app), 28 May 2025).
- **Plans (names only):** **The X Subscription** tiers Premium, Super and Ultra (announced 12 Oct 2026). Also legacy SuperGrok / SuperGrok Plus / SuperGrok Heavy / X Premium+, legacy Cursor paid plans and Grok Business. Source: [docs.x.ai overview](https://docs.x.ai/grok-bot/overview) and [FAQ](https://docs.x.ai/grok-bot/faq). Earlier plan list: [x.ai, more plans](https://x.ai/news/grok-bot-more-plans) (26 Aug 2026).
- **India:** xAI publishes **no country list** for Grok Bot. Grok itself is available in India. [BusinessToday](https://www.businesstoday.in/technology/news/story/claude-duet-vs-grok-bot-vs-chatgpt-work-vs-meta-muse-which-ai-agent-is-worth-using-in-india-563122-2026-10-22) (23 Oct 2026) does not confirm India availability either.

## 3. Meta Muse / Muse Spark / Meta AI

- **Muse Spark (model):** first Muse model from Meta Superintelligence Labs, live **8 Apr 2026** on meta.ai and in the Meta AI app, then in WhatsApp, Instagram, Facebook and Messenger. Source: [about.fb.com](https://about.fb.com/news/2026/04/introducing-muse-spark-meta-superintelligence-labs/) (8 Apr 2026).
- **Muse (agent app):** launched **8 Sep 2026** in the US on iOS and Android. It "actually does the work": sending email, booking travel, and long-running goals. It runs on a private "Muse Secure VM". It is free for most things, with paid subscription plans (names not given). Source: [about.fb.com, Introducing Muse](https://about.fb.com/news/2026/09/introducing-muse-personal-ai-agent/) (8 Sep 2026). Opening a browser, filling forms and negotiating are covered by [PBS/AP](https://www.pbs.org/newshour/nation/meta-launches-personal-ai-agent-muse-to-help-with-everyday-tasks).
- **Where you use it:** **the Muse app, WhatsApp, and the web at muse.ai**, with AI glasses coming. Source: [about.fb.com](https://about.fb.com/news/2026/09/introducing-muse-personal-ai-agent/). So **Muse does work inside WhatsApp**: you message it like a contact. Third-party sites say that in WhatsApp it is text only, with no live voice and no calls ([builtwithmuse.com](https://builtwithmuse.com/blog/muse-voice-chat), Oct 2026).
- **Countries:** US (8 Sep), **Canada** (18 Sep, [iPhone in Canada](https://www.iphoneincanada.ca/2026/09/18/metas-muse-ai-agent-is-now-available-in-canada/)), and **Singapore** (27 Oct 2026, [about.fb.com](https://about.fb.com/news/2026/10/muse-is-now-available-in-singapore-the-worlds-first-personal-ai-agent-built-for-everyone/)). **Not in India.** "Meta has also not announced an India release date for Muse" ([BusinessToday](https://www.businesstoday.in/technology/news/story/claude-duet-vs-grok-bot-vs-chatgpt-work-vs-meta-muse-which-ai-agent-is-worth-using-in-india-563122-2026-10-22), 23 Oct 2026).
- **Calls it makes:** Muse gained "the ability to place calls to U.S. businesses" ([TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/09/17/rival-ai-agents-instinct-and-metas-muse-both-add-the-ability-to-make-calls/), 17 Sep 2026).
- **Rollback:** Reuters reported on 22 Sep 2026 that some Muse calls were secretly made by **human contractors** (a "human concierge" test). Meta said it had "rolled back this feature", meaning the **contractor-placed calls**, not necessarily all AI calls ([Reuters via KSL](https://www.ksl.com/article/51627159/exclusive-meta-testing-a-human-concierge-for-its-new-personal-ai-agent-muse); [TechSpot](https://www.techspot.com/news/113957-meta-muse-ai-used-human-contractors-calls-including.html)).
- **Meta AI voice inside WhatsApp:** you press the waveform button and talk to Meta AI, and it talks back. This is a **voice mode inside the chat, not a phone call** to an agent. Source: [WhatsApp Blog](https://blog.whatsapp.com/talk-to-meta-ai-on-whatsapp) (25 Sep 2024). A newer beta keeps the voice session open across apps ([Green-API](https://green-api.com/en/blog/2026/whatsapp-is-rolling-out-voice-chats-powered-by-meta-ai/), third-party, 2026).

## 4. Other AIs you can call in India + WhatsApp policy

- **Jio Call Agent:** an AI built into the network. You say "Hey Jio" during a normal call, in 22 Indian languages. It was announced at the Reliance AGM on 19 Jun 2026 for "later in 2026", and launch status is not confirmed ([Beebom](https://gadgets.beebom.com/news/reliance-jio-ai-call-agent-myjio-ai-advisor-announced-agm-2026)). Note that it joins calls; you do not call it to hand off work.
- **Perplexity on WhatsApp:** ended 15 Jan 2026, and no phone line was found ([Gulf News](https://gulfnews.com/technology/no-more-chatgpt-and-perplexity-on-whatsapp-meta-bans-major-ai-chatbots-from-2026-1.500316016)).
- **Bolna AI:** offers a demo voice agent on an Indian number, but it is a business call-centre tool, not a personal agent ([bolna.ai](https://www.bolna.ai/)).
- **WhatsApp Business policy:** from **15 Jan 2026**, "AI Providers" may not use the WhatsApp Business Platform when general-purpose AI is the **primary** function. Businesses using AI for their own customer service are allowed. Source: [WhatsApp Business Solution Terms](https://www.whatsapp.com/legal/business-solution-terms) and [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2025/10/18/whatssapp-changes-its-terms-to-bar-general-purpose-chatbots-from-its-platform/) (18 Oct 2025).
- **Exceptions:** Meta's official list covers **Italy** (16 Feb 2026), **Brazil** and the **EEA countries** (11 Mar 2026), and **Türkiye** (15 Oct 2026). These are paid "AI Provider" pricing. **India is not on the list.** Source: [developers.facebook.com, AI Providers pricing](https://developers.facebook.com/documentation/business-messaging/whatsapp/pricing/ai-providers.md) (accessed 2026-10-28). No CCI (India) action found.
