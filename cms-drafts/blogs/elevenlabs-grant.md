On 14 November 2026, ElevenLabs accepted Classgrid into the ElevenLabs Startup Grants programme. We are a bootstrapped company from India with no outside funding. ElevenLabs looked at the product alone and said yes. That is why this one means so much to us.

Classgrid is an AI workspace for institutions and students: chat, Studio, projects and Grids. Until now, Classgrid AI has mostly worked in text. This grant gives it a voice, and it lets it make pictures and video.

## What ElevenLabs gives us

The grant runs for a year, until 14 November 2027. In that time Classgrid gets:

- 33 million ElevenLabs credits for the ElevenAPI. That covers text to speech with the v4 voices in more than 90 languages, including Hindi, and speech to text with Scribe.
- 1 million image and video credits. ElevenLabs' image and video API fronts GPT Image, Gemini image and Seedream for pictures, and Veo 3.1 and Seedance for video.
- ElevenAgents on the Scale plan: 3,738 call minutes a month, with 30 calls at the same time, for voice agents.

## What it means for people who use Classgrid

Here is what we are building with it.

**Replies you can listen to.** Classgrid AI will read its replies aloud in natural voices. A student can hear an explanation instead of reading it. A teacher can listen to a summary while doing something else. Hindi is one of the languages, which matters a lot for the institutions we serve.

**Speak instead of type.** Voice messages and voice input will be typed out by Scribe. Ask a question out loud and Classgrid AI gets the text. Send a voice note and it comes back as words you can search and reuse.

**Voice calls on WhatsApp.** With ElevenAgents, Classgrid will be able to take and make voice calls on WhatsApp. A parent can call and ask about a notice. A student can call and get help with a topic. The call is handled by a voice agent that knows your institution's Classgrid.

**Pictures and video inside Classgrid AI.** Ask for a diagram, a poster, a cover image or a short clip, and Classgrid AI makes it right there in the conversation.

All of this lands in the same Classgrid AI, wherever you use it: chat.classgrid.in, the Classgrid desktop app, Classgrid Code, and the Classgrid extension for VS Code and other editors. One AI, the same everywhere.

We are wiring these into the product now. We will not promise dates. We would rather ship each piece when it works well than announce a day and rush it. You will see them appear in your Classgrid as they are ready, and we will write about each one here.

## Thank you

To the ElevenLabs team and the Startups programme: thank you. You took a small, self-funded team from India seriously, looked at what we had built, and backed it. We will put every credit to good use.

If you are a student or an institution on Classgrid, the next few months are going to be fun. If you are not yet, this is a good time to have a look.
