# Classgrid Milestones

A living record of every milestone in Classgrid's journey.

Classgrid builds Classgrid AI, an AI agent that answers questions, researches the web, works with your files and runs tasks for you, with results on WhatsApp.

---

## 📱 Remote PC — Your Computer, From Your Phone
**Date:** November 18, 2026  
**Status:** ✅ Released  

Classgrid released **Remote PC**. Type `/remote` in a chat in the Classgrid desktop app or Classgrid Code, and you can carry on that computer chat from the Classgrid phone app: send new messages, answer the AI's questions and give your OK when the computer needs it. The phone also shows the chat's **Review**, **Files** and **Terminal**, so you can see what the AI changed and what it ran on your computer.

## ✉️ Automated Multi-Provider Email & Resend Fallback
**Date:** July 23, 2026  
**Status:** ✅ Live & Deployed  

Finalized and deployed Classgrid's automated dual-provider marketing & transactional email engine:
1. **Amazon SES (Stockholm):** Dedicated engine for high-volume platform transactional emails (OTPs, login alerts, payment receipts) with 50,000/day capacity via $1,000 AWS Activate credits.
2. **Brevo + Resend Smart Fallback (Cron Engine):** Blog & changelog subscriber notifications send up to 250 emails/day via Brevo. If Brevo hits daily rate limits (421/429), the Vercel cron job automatically seamlessly switches to **Resend** via `updates.classgrid.in` for the next 100 emails/day — achieving **350 emails/day for ₹0**.
