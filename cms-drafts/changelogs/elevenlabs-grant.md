ElevenLabs has selected Classgrid for an ElevenLabs Grant. We were accepted into the [ElevenLabs Startup Grants](https://elevenlabs.io/startup-grants) programme on 14 November 2026. Classgrid is bootstrapped and has no outside funding. The grant was given on the product alone.

## What the grant includes

The grant runs for a year, until 14 November 2027. It gives us:

- 33 million ElevenLabs credits for the ElevenAPI. That covers text to speech with the v4 voices in more than 90 languages, including Hindi, and speech to text with Scribe.
- 1 million image and video credits. The ElevenLabs image and video API fronts GPT Image, Gemini image and Seedream for pictures, and Veo 3.1 and Seedance for video.
- ElevenAgents on the Scale plan, with 3,738 call minutes a month and 30 calls at the same time, for voice agents.

## What changes in Classgrid

We are wiring the grant into Classgrid AI now. This is what it brings:

- Voice replies read aloud by natural voices.
- Voice messages and voice input typed out by Scribe.
- Voice calls on WhatsApp through ElevenAgents.
- Picture and video generation inside Classgrid AI.

Classgrid AI is the same across chat.classgrid.in, the Classgrid desktop app, Classgrid Code and the Classgrid extension for VS Code and other editors. Each of these will reach all of them once it is ready. We are not promising dates. We will post here as each part goes live.

## Thank you

We thank the ElevenLabs team for choosing Classgrid. You can read about the programme at [ElevenLabs Startup Grants](https://elevenlabs.io/startup-grants).
